M01 · AUTONOMOUS DEPLOYMENT

Before people arrive,
the habitat will be waiting.

Selene M01 will deliver and commission a self-sustaining surface habitat near the lunar south pole. The system lands, unfolds and begins producing power—without a crew on site.

Every component is built around one idea: the first home beyond Earth should be ready before its residents leave theirs.
