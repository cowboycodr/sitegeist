Sunday Supper began with a borrowed table, a roast in the oven, and more friends than chairs. We’ve grown a little since then, but the idea is the same: cook with the season, open something delicious, and make everyone feel at home.

Our farmers are nearby, our wines are made with a light touch, and our hospitality comes with an extra spoon.
