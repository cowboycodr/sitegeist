Curatorial note

Against disappearance

Not every photograph enters history cleanly. Some arrive creased, mislabelled, partially fixed, or without a name. Afterimage Archive attends to these unstable objects—not as errors, but as evidence of photography thinking aloud.

Our holdings bring together private donations, rescued studio material, and artist-led experiments made between 1968 and 1999. Records remain provisional by design. If you recognize a place, hand, or process, the archive invites your knowledge.
