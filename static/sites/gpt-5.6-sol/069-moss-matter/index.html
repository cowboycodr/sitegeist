The growing room

Nature does
the hard work.

We create the conditions. Mycelium does the making—binding local fibers into strong, lightweight forms without petrochemicals.

  1. 01

    Source

    Regional farm waste is cleaned and prepared.

  2. 02

    Grow

    Mycelium binds the fibers in precision molds.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Heat and time create a stable, durable material.

  4. 04

    Return

    After use, nutrients cycle safely back to soil.
