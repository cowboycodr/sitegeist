ARCHIVE / FIELD NOTE 06

After the dry season

35.4° S — 149.1° E / 18:42

The wind moved through waist-high grass, carrying the soft bitterness of crushed stems. Far off, a controlled burn drew a charcoal line across the sky. Then rain: three drops, perhaps four, enough to wake the stone.

That meeting—heat, root, mineral, smoke—became the first accord of Veldt No. 06.
