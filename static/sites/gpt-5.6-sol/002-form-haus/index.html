Form follows feeling

A disciplined practice.
A human point of view.

Form Haus is an architecture studio founded in Copenhagen in 2012. We work across homes, cultural spaces, and considered hospitality.

Our process begins by removing. We listen for what a place asks of us, then reduce each idea until structure, light, and use become one clear gesture. The result is architecture that is generous without excess.
