Atelier

Composed between dusk and first light

Our perfumers work only at night, when the nose is quiet and the materials speak plainly. Tinctures rest in darkness for a full lunar month before blending; nothing is rushed toward the light.

  • 28 nights of maceration for every batch
  • Hand-drawn extraits, numbered by hour and moon phase
  • No brightness added — no citrus openings, no daylight musks
