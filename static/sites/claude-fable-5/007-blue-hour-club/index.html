The regulars

Resident artists

Four musicians hold standing nights here. They set the room's temperature; everyone else plays against it.

  • Mondays

    Ida Vann

    Piano

    Plays standards like she's remembering them wrong on purpose. The quietest Monday in the city, and the hardest ticket.

  • Wednesdays

    Terrell Okafor

    Bass

    Leads a trio without a chordal instrument and dares you to miss it. Long tones, long pauses, long memory.

  • Thursdays

    June Castellano

    Voice

    Sings the verses nobody learns anymore. Keeps a notebook of requests and honors about one a month.

  • Sundays

    Ray Halvorsen

    Drums

    Runs the Sunday session: open bandstand, closed fists, brushes only until midnight. Bring your horn and your nerve.

Low light, close walls

The room

Eighty seats, a low tin ceiling, and a bandstand close enough to read the charts. We keep the lights at candle level and the conversation at listening level — during the music, the room belongs to the band.

Between sets is another story. The bar runs late, the espresso machine runs later, and the best arguments about music in Chicago happen at the back tables around two in the morning.

  • Where — 1147 N. Larrabee, lower level. Look for the blue lamp.
  • When — Doors 9 PM, seven nights. Music until the music stops.
  • House rules — Phones dark during sets. Applause encouraged mid-tune.
