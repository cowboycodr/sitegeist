How a session works

No algorithms, no shuffle, no skipping ahead alone. Three small rules make the room feel like a living room.

  1. 01

    Pick the record

    The host chooses one album and posts the door time. Friends get a sleeve-note preview and a seat at the table.

  2. 02

    Drop the needle together

    Playback is synced to the second for everyone. While a track plays, chat softens to whispers so the music leads.

  3. 03

    Talk in the grooves

    Between tracks the floor opens: hot takes, liner-note trivia, and the next discovery someone can’t wait to queue.
