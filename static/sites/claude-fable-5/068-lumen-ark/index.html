Lumen Ark is a Copenhagen studio creating quiet, daylight-led spaces for culture and living. We begin every project with the sun — its path, its seasons, its northern reluctance — and let rooms take their form from it.

Selected work

Projects

Four buildings, four ways of borrowing the Nordic sky. Each was drawn first as a study of shadow, then as a plan.

  • Havnegalleri

    Cultural — Copenhagen harbour, 2025

    A pair of vaulted exhibition halls turned to face the water, where reflected harbour light reaches deep into the galleries and no artwork ever needs a lamp before noon.

  • Solhus Terraces

    Housing — Nordhavn, 2024

    Forty-one homes stepped around a shared ridge lantern. Each stair, kitchen and winter garden is placed on the sunny side of the section, so daylight does the wayfinding.

  • Læselund Library

    Civic — Valby, 2023

    A reading room beneath a shallow oculus dome. The light pool drifts across the tables through the day, and readers follow it like sundial marks.

  • Kalk Chapel

    Sacred — Møn, 2022

    Three limewashed sawtooth bays open only to the north-east, catching sunrise on Easter morning and holding a cool, even glow for the rest of the year.

How we work

Approach

  1. 01

    Read the sky

    Before a single wall is drawn we chart a year of sun paths, cloud cover and neighbouring shadows on the site. In Denmark, daylight is a budget — we count it before we spend it.

  2. 02

    Shape the section

    Plans follow sections here. Vaults, lanterns and stepped floors are sized by physical daylight models, not by habit, so every principal room earns its window.

  3. 03

    Build quietly

    Lime, timber and pale brick take the light softly and age well. We detail for stillness: few materials, honest joints, and rooms that need almost nothing added to feel complete.

Writing

Studio notes

Short field notes from the drawing board, the site hut and the long Danish dusk.

  • 21 June 2026

    Midsummer measurements

    At solstice we measured seventeen and a half hours of usable daylight in the Havnegalleri vaults. The evening light arrives horizontal and copper-coloured; the morning light is grey-blue and vertical. The building holds both without complaint.

  • 9 March 2026

    On borrowed light

    A corridor should never own its own window. At Solhus every hallway borrows light through glazed transoms from the rooms it serves, which keeps it modest — and keeps you moving toward the brighter place.

  • 30 November 2025

    The november test

    We judge every scheme by its worst afternoon: an overcast Sunday in late November. If a room feels generous then, June will take care of itself. Kalk Chapel passed this year at 15:41, just before the light went.

The studio

Studio

Lumen Ark was founded in 2016 in a former sailmaker's loft on Refshaleøen. We are twelve architects, one model-maker and a daylight lab of mirrors, heliodons and paper. We take on a small number of cultural and residential commissions each year, in Denmark and across the Nordics.

We are always glad to talk about slow buildings and northern light.
