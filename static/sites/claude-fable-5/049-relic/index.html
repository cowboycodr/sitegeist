How Relic works

Provenance before price

Nothing is listed until we can say where it lived, who made it, and why it lasted.

  1. I

    Unearthed

    Our buyers walk estate clearances, workshop closures, and attic sales across Europe, choosing perhaps one object in fifty.

  2. II

    Examined

    Each piece is dated, tested, and honestly graded — Weathered, Worn well, or Restored — with every flaw noted in the listing.

  3. III

    Entrusted

    Objects ship crated with their provenance card. If a relic doesn't earn its place in your room within thirty days, send it home.
