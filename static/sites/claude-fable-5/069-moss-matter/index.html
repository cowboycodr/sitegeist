The cycle

From field, to form, to field again

  1. 01

    Collect

    We gather agricultural residues — hemp hurd, spent brewery grain, corn stover — from farms within 200 km of the studio.

  2. 02

    Inoculate

    The substrate is pasteurized and seeded with mycelium strains selected for density, flexibility, or acoustic grain.

  3. 03

    Grow

    In dark, humid grow rooms the mycelium binds the waste into solid form inside reusable molds — no heat, no pressure, no resin.

  4. 04

    Return

    After a useful life, every piece breaks down in a garden compost heap and feeds the next season's soil.

Notes from the grow room

Field journal

  • June 2026 · Entry 41

    Reishi outperforms oyster on flex

    Our reishi-bound Sporeweave sheets held a 180° fold for six weeks without cracking. Oyster-strain sheets stiffened at week two. Reishi becomes the house strain for textiles.

  • April 2026 · Entry 38

    Compost trial, backyard conditions

    Twelve Mycoform corner blocks buried in an ordinary garden heap: full structural breakdown by day 28, no visible fragments by day 34. Soil pH unchanged.

  • February 2026 · Entry 35

    Hypha Panels in a listening room

    A local radio studio lined its booth with 60 panels. Measured reverberation time dropped from 0.8 s to 0.4 s — matching mineral wool, minus the mineral wool.
