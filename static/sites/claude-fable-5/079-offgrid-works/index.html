One chassis, many lives

Swap the interior like luggage.

Every Offgrid Works camper is built on a flat skateboard battery deck with a rail-mounted interior. Modules lock in with four latches each — reconfigure the whole cabin in an afternoon, no tools heavier than your hands.

  • Galley

    Induction two-burner, 65 L fridge drawer, and a cutting-board counter that folds out the side door for camp cooking.

  • Bunk

    A true queen platform with a cedar-slat base, plus a drop-down single up top when the whole crew comes along.

  • Desk

    A standing-height workstation with a second display arm, sound-damped walls, and a window that frames the horizon, not the parking lot.

  • Gear locker

    Slide-out ski and board racks, a drying cabinet with vented airflow, and lashing rails for anything with wheels.

  • Wet bath

    Recirculating shower that stretches 110 L of water across a week, with a composting head and heated teak floor.

  • Power wall

    An extra 8 kWh module that feeds tools, e-bikes, or your house in an outage — and charges back off the roof by noon.

A day beyond signal.

  1. 06:40

    Wake to weather, not alarms

    The roof glass dims itself at dusk and clears at dawn. Diffused light, no LEDs blinking, cabin already warmed by the heat pump.

  2. 08:15

    The desk unfolds

    Bunk stows flat, desk module rotates out. Sound-damped panels keep calls crisp even when rain is working the roof.

  3. 12:30

    Lunch off the tailgate

    The galley counter slides through the side door. Induction burner, fridge drawer, and a view you didn't have to book.

  4. 17:00

    Move camp on sunlight

    The 1.2 kW roof array put 30 miles back in the pack while you worked. Quiet dual motors mean the drive is part of the rest.

  5. 21:10

    Lights out, heat on

    The battery deck carries the night with the heat pump running and margin to spare. Tomorrow, the map is optional again.
