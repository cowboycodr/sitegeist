01 The cartridge shelf

Three worlds in active development. Loud, small, and tuned until the controller hums.

Magma Hop

Vertical platformer

Bounce a lava slime up a collapsing volcano. One button, ninety-nine near misses per minute.

Status: polishing the wobble

Neon Driftline

Micro racer

Slide chunky hovercars through a city drawn in four shades of loud. Ghost your friends, then your past self.

Status: tuning tire squeal

Sprout & Smash

Garden brawler

Grow vegetables by day, defend them by night with a watering can that doubles as a bat. Co-op on one keyboard.

Status: balancing the beets

03 Meet the crew

Four humans, one shared palette, far too many arcade sticks.

Juno Park

Direction & design

Decides which three mechanics survive the cut. Keeps a jar of rejected power-ups on the desk.

Mabel Osei

Pixel art

Paints entire biomes sixteen pixels at a time. Believes every sprite deserves an idle animation.

Rex Calder

Code & game feel

Measures fun in milliseconds of hitstop. Will not ship a jump that feels floaty. Has never once felt floaty.

Suki Tanaka

Chiptune & sound

Writes boss themes in three channels and one triangle wave. Hums in square waves when concentrating.
