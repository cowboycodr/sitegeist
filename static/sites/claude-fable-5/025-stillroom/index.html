Explore practices

Each practice is a simple, well-worn shape of breath. Begin one directly, or read a little first — there is no wrong door.

Resonant tide

5.5 in · 5.5 out

Breathing near six breaths a minute, where heart and lungs settle into an easy conversation. The steady default for daily practice.

Box breathing

4 in · 4 hold · 4 out · 4 hold

Four even sides — in, held, out, held. A composed, square rhythm for gathering yourself before or after a demanding hour.

Long exhale

4 in · 7 hold · 8 out

A brief inhale, a quiet pause, then an exhale twice as long. The extended out-breath asks the body, kindly, to stand down.

Grounding

4 in · 6 out

In for four, out for six — a slight lean toward the exhale. Light enough to practice anywhere, with eyes open, feet on the floor.

Made around spacious pacing

Stillroom keeps a few quiet commitments, so the room stays a room and never becomes a feed.

  • Nothing counts you

    No streaks, scores, or reminders. A practice ends when it ends, and the room does not remember whether you came yesterday.

  • Form follows breath

    The vessel widens and settles at the true tempo of each practice — the interface breathes with you rather than at you.

  • Words can carry it

    When your device asks for reduced motion, the session moves to plain, unhurried language. The practice loses nothing.
