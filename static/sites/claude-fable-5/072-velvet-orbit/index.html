Atelier

Made where the light is slow

Every Velvet Orbit piece is cut once, by hand, from cloth woven for us in runs of forty metres.

Tailoring
Sculpted canvas fronts, set by iron and time — no fusing, ever.
Textiles
Liquid metallic weaves developed with two mills; each bolt is numbered.
Fittings
By appointment at the atelier. Three sittings, one orbit apart.
Provenance
A woven ring inside every garment records its look number and bolt.
