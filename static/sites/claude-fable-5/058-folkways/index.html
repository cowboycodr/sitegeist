About the archive

Kept in trust

Folkways holds several thousand hours of tape, wax, wire, and digital sound, collected over seven decades and catalogued alongside the stories of the people who made it. Nothing here is background music: every item names its singer, its place, and — wherever the community wishes — the terms on which it may be heard.

Recordings return home first. Communities receive copies, corrections, and the final word on access before anything enters the public listening room.
