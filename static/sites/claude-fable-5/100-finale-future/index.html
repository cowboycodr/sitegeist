03 / Beyond the interface

Conversations

Small rooms, long tables, no slides. Each conversation seats twelve people and one question, and ends when the question does.

Does software have a body?

Choreographers and systems programmers on gesture, weight, and the physics of code.

Who hears a network?

Field recordists and infrastructure engineers listen to the hum beneath the cloud.

Is the future still strange?

Writers and toolmakers ask whether we automated the surprise out of tomorrow.

What survives a format?

Archivists and artists on keeping work alive after its medium dies.

04 / Admission

Enter the festival

Finale is free and distributed. Streams, scores, and source files publish at each day's opening; local nodes host the night programs wherever three or more people gather with a screen.

  • Everywhere. No venue, no capacity, no queue. If you can render a page, you're inside.
  • October 08–10. Three days only. Nothing is recorded after the closing performance.
  • One hundred visions. The full archive is open for exactly seventy-two hours.

Doors open 10:00 local time, Wednesday October 08.
