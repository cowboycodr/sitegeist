Good to know

Questions, answered

Is Orbit a real bank?

Orbit is a fictional showcase brand. In this concept, deposits would be held with partner banks and insured up to applicable limits in each region.

What does an account cost?

The standard Orbit account is free — no monthly fees, no minimum balance. An optional Orbit+ tier adds higher ATM limits and travel insurance.

How fast are international transfers?

Most transfers between Orbit users land instantly. Transfers to outside accounts typically arrive the same day within supported corridors.

Can I hold multiple currencies at once?

Yes. Hold and exchange 40+ currencies in one account, and spend from any balance with the same card.
