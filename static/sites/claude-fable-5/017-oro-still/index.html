Our materials

We work with three metals only, each chosen for how it ages in your keeping rather than how it looks in a case.

I. Cast bronze

Poured from reclaimed Tuscan bronze, then left unlacquered. It darkens where you don’t touch it and brightens where you do, recording the habit of your hands.

II. Sterling silver

Fully recycled sterling, brushed rather than polished. We like the way silver keeps a diary of small scratches and asks nothing in return but wearing.

III. 18k gold

Remelted from old jewelry brought to us by clients and dealers. Every gold piece carries earlier lives inside it — which is, we think, the whole point of gold.

The atelier

Lost-wax casting has not meaningfully changed in four thousand years. Neither has our patience with it.

  1. Carve. Each form is cut freehand from jeweler’s wax at the bench, never modeled on a screen.
  2. Invest. The wax is entombed in plaster and burned away overnight, leaving its ghost as a mold.
  3. Pour. Molten metal fills the void at dawn — the only step that takes seconds instead of days.
  4. Finish. Files, stones, and cloth, in that order, until the object stops feeling made and starts feeling found.
