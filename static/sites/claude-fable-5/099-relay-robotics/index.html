Meet Relay One

A waist-high courier that navigates crowded hallways, rides elevators on its own, and hands off deliveries with a smile — well, a soft chime and a friendly screen.

  • Always on time

    Relay One plans around foot traffic in real time, averaging door-to-door runs 40% faster than a staffed round trip.

  • Locked and logged

    The cargo bay opens only for the intended recipient — via badge, PIN, or room key — and every handoff is time-stamped.

  • Elevator fluent

    Native integrations with major elevator and door systems let one robot serve every floor of your building, unattended.

  • Gentle by design

    360° sensing, a polite pace, and a yielding personality mean Relay One is safe around wheelchairs, gurneys, and toddlers.
