The Lucid method

See it. Poke it. Predict it.

  1. 1

    See the idea

    Every lesson opens with a picture, not a paragraph. The concept is on screen before a single symbol appears.

  2. 2

    Poke it yourself

    Sliders, drags, and toggles let you break the system on purpose. Intuition comes from the edge cases you find.

  3. 3

    Predict what happens

    Before revealing an answer, Lucid asks you to commit to a guess. Being confidently wrong is the fastest way to remember being right.
