For researchers

Research residencies

Two-to-twelve-week residencies for astronomers, atmospheric scientists, and instrument builders who need real darkness and a warm place to think.

Instruments

A 1.0 m Ritchey–Chrétien reflector, a 0.5 m wide-field astrograph, an all-sky auroral imaging array, and bench space for visiting hardware. Dedicated fiber between domes and the data barn.

Life at the field

Six private cabins, a shared kitchen and sauna, and a night-shifted rhythm: quiet hours run through the daylight so observers sleep well. Meals appear when the sky clouds over.

How to apply

We review proposals twice a year, at the equinoxes. A residency covers lodging, telescope time, and technical support; travel grants are available for early-career researchers.

For everyone

Public nights

On the darkest weekends of the season we open the gates. Dress warmly; the universe does not heat the viewing terrace.

  1. 20:00 Arrival & dark adaptation

    Headlights off at the lower lot. A red-lit trail leads up the ridge while your eyes learn what darkness actually means.

  2. 21:00 The naked-eye sky

    A guided tour of the season's constellations, satellites, meteors, and — most nights — the shifting curtains of the aurora itself.

  3. 22:30 At the eyepiece

    Small groups rotate through both domes: planets and the Moon in the astrograph, galaxies and nebulae in the one-metre reflector.

  4. 00:00 Fire, cocoa, questions

    The night ends around the fire pit with resident astronomers on hand. No question about the universe is too small or too large.

Getting here

Plan a visit

The field sits at the end of a 34 km gravel road north of the Kaldá river crossing. The journey is part of the point.

Season

Public nights run late August through mid-April, whenever astronomical darkness returns. Midsummer brings the midnight sun — beautiful, but useless for astronomy — so summer visits are daytime tours of the instruments only.

What to bring

Layered clothing rated well below freezing, insulated boots, a red flashlight if you have one, and patience. Leave white lights, drones, and haste at home. Loaner parkas are available at the gate.

Access

The trail from the lower lot to the terrace is 600 m with a gentle grade; a heated shuttle serves visitors who prefer not to walk. Both domes and the fire circle are step-free.

Weather policy

If the ridge clouds over, the night moves indoors: a live tour of the control room and the auroral archive. Cloud-night tickets carry over to any clear night in the same season.
