Weekly rides

Same week, four moods

All rides roll from Vippetangen pier unless noted. No-drop means no-drop — the group waits at every summit.

  • Tue18:00

    Hour of Power

    Fast, flat chain-gang along the fjord. Rotating pairs, 40 km, average 34 km/h. Front lights required after September.

    Fast
  • Thu17:30

    Hills & Coffee

    Two ascents of the Tryvann Ramp at your own tempo, regroup at the tower, descend together to Frogner for espresso.

    No-drop
  • Sat09:00

    The Long Road

    Our signature exploratory ride. A new 90–130 km route each week, published Thursday night. Pace conversational, distance not.

    Explore
  • Sun10:00

    Forest Slow Roll

    Gravel social through Nordmarka. Starts at Sognsvann. Thermos mandatory, heart-rate monitors discouraged.

    Social
