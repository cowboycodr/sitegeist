Three nights

Each night has a name.

01 · Fri Sep 18

Ignition

The gates open as the sun drops. Four stages light in sequence across the flats, ending with Nova Cathedral under a wall of strobing heat lamps.

02 · Sat Sep 19

Corona

The loudest day of the year. Marathon sets, the Heat Index brass line marching between stages, and a sunrise show for the ones still standing.

03 · Sun Sep 20

Afterglow

Everything softens. Mirror Dunes close the main stage while the art field burns low and the desert takes the last word.

Radiant art

The field glows back.

Twelve commissioned works scattered across a half-mile of open desert, each one built to be walked into, climbed on, or lit from within.

  • Heliograph

    A forty-foot dish of salvaged mirrors that catches the last daylight and releases it, slowly, until midnight.

  • Signal Garden

    Two hundred antenna flowers that hum in chords when the wind crosses the wash.

  • The Long Now Bonfire

    A choreographed flame sculpture that burns one careful hour per night, never the same shape twice.

  • Night Without Edges

    An open pavilion of blackout fabric and pinprick light — the piece the festival is named for.

Know before you glow

Desert rules.

Where
Flare Flats, Joshua Tree, California — 20 minutes north of the park entrance, signposted from Highway 62.
When
Gates open Friday Sep 18 at 4:00 pm. Music ends Sunday Sep 20 at 2:00 am; camp clears by noon Monday.
Bring
A refillable bottle, dust protection, warm layers for after midnight, and shoes you don't love.
Leave
Nothing. The flats go back to the tortoises exactly as we found them.
