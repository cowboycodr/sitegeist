Beautiful noise, carefully released.

Static Bloom is a fiercely independent label pressing chromatic records in small, deliberate runs — music that hisses, shimmers, and refuses to sit still.

Latest release · SB-018

Marrow Chorus — Petal Static

Nine tracks of blown-out harmonium and tape-warped choir, recorded in a decommissioned grain silo over one humid August. Our loudest quiet record yet.

A thirty-second generative texture, synthesized right here in your browser. No files, no streaming — just noise, blooming.
