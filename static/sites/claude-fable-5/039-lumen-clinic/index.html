How it works

Three quiet steps, four times a year.

  1. 01

    Draw

    A 20-minute visit at a Lumen studio or a nurse at your door. One small draw covers the full seasonal panel — no fasting marathons, no surprise add-ons.

  2. 02

    Decode

    Within five days, your results arrive translated into plain language, mapped against your own history rather than a population average.

  3. 03

    Direct

    A 45-minute review with your clinician turns the panel into two or three specific, unhurried changes worth making before next season.

Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. Lumen sits alongside your primary care. We hand you — and, with your consent, your physician — a clearer longitudinal picture than an annual visit can capture.

What happens if a result is concerning?

Your clinician contacts you within one business day, explains what the number does and does not mean, and coordinates follow-up testing or a referral without alarmism.

Who can see my data?

You and your Lumen clinician. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising — ever. You can export or delete your full record at any time.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes, anytime, from your account. Your baseline and history are kept intact so a pause never costs you your trend lines.
