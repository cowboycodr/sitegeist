Three quiet ingredients

Drift doesn't gamify your rest or grade your night. It listens to how your evening is going and offers just enough — a sound, a breath, a small ritual — then gets out of the way.

Adaptive sound

Layered soundscapes that soften as you settle. Rain thins to mist, waves lengthen, tones sink lower — mirroring your wind-down instead of looping at you.

Guided breath

A slow visual pacer for evening breathing — four counts in, six counts out. Two minutes is usually enough to trade the day's tempo for your own.

Screen-free routines

Short, printed-feel rituals you do away from the phone: dim one lamp, warm one drink, write one line. Drift cues them, then goes dark on purpose.

A wind-down that fits in one hour

Drift's evening arc is deliberately small. Each step is a nudge, not a task — and the last one happens with the screen face-down.

  1. T−60

    Lights step down

    Drift suggests dimming one light in the room you're actually in. Warmth over darkness — the goal is dusk, not a cave.

  2. T−40

    Sound comes up

    Your chosen soundscape fades in at a whisper and slowly simplifies, shedding layers as the hour goes on.

  3. T−20

    Two minutes of breath

    The pacer opens for a short session. In for four, out for six — long exhales tell your nervous system the day is over.

  4. T−10

    Screen goes dark

    Drift dims to a single ember of light, then nothing. Sound carries on without the screen. The rest of the landing is yours.
