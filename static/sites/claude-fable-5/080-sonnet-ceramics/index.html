From cliff to cupboard

How a piece is made

Each vessel spends about six weeks with us before it leaves. Nothing here is rushed, because clay remembers every shortcut.

  1. 01

    Wedge & throw

    Iron-rich clay is wedged by hand and thrown on a slow wheel, one form at a time, walls left a touch thick for turning.

  2. 02

    Rest & trim

    Pieces rest under cloth until leather-hard, then are trimmed to their final weight and signed on the foot.

  3. 03

    Glaze & wax

    Glazes are mixed in the studio from ash and feldspar; rims are dipped twice, feet waxed clean.

  4. 04

    Fire & keep

    A thirteen-hour firing to cone six, a slow cool, and an honest sort: only the pieces we would use ourselves are kept.
