The listening

Silence is the canvas.

Vanta begins where most headphones stop: with the quiet. A sealed acoustic chamber and matched planar drivers hold the noise floor so low that reverb tails, room tone, and the breath before a phrase come back into the recording.

Depth

Front-to-back staging you can walk around in. Instruments hold their position at any volume, so a mix unfolds as a space, not a wall.

Texture

Sub-millisecond transient settling preserves grain — bow on string, breath on reed, the fingertips on a fretboard between notes.

Weight

Bass tuned for pressure, not bloom. Low end lands in the chest and leaves the midrange untouched, the way a room feels it.

The engineering

Built around a single moving part.

A planar diaphragm three microns thick, suspended in a symmetric magnet array. No voice-coil mass, no break-up modes — just a film of air made precise.

  1. 01

    50 mm planar magnetic driver

    The full surface moves as one plane. Push-pull neodymium arrays on both sides keep drive force uniform to the diaphragm's edge.

  2. 02

    Sealed carbon acoustic chamber

    A carbon-fiber rear volume tuned like an instrument body, damping cup resonance without swallowing decay.

  3. 03

    Pair-matched at the bench

    Left and right drivers are measured together and matched within 0.4 dB before a serial number is engraved.

  4. 04

    Phase-true crossoverless path

    One driver, no crossover, no DSP in the signal path. What arrives at your ear is time-aligned by physics, not correction.

The materials

Matte black, warmed by brass.

Every surface is chosen for how it sounds, then finished for how it wears. Vanta is meant to be handled for decades.

Machined aluminum

Cups milled from a single billet, bead-blasted and anodized to a light-absorbing matte black.

Solid brass yokes

Weighted pivots that settle the cups against the head and patina slowly with your hands.

Lambskin & memory foam

Slow-recovery pads seal gently over glasses and hair, holding the acoustic chamber consistent.
