01The menu

Three pots. One table. Everything gets dipped eventually.

Moitié-Moitié

The classic

Gruyère and Vacherin Fribourgeois, white wine, a rude amount of garlic, and a splash of kirsch nobody admits to.

Cheddar & Ale

The loud one

Sharp farmhouse cheddar folded into dark ale with mustard and smoked paprika. Pairs well with poor decisions.

The Chocolate Finale

The alibi

Dark chocolate, a whisper of orange, and whatever fruit survived the ride over. Arrives when you least deserve it.

For dipping

  • Day-old sourdough cubes
  • Baby potatoes, still steaming
  • Cornichons & pickled onions
  • Charred broccoli spears
  • Apple wedges (controversial)
  • Whatever you smuggle in

02House rules

  1. Lose your bread in the pot, you pour the next round of wine. Ancient law. Non-negotiable.
  2. Phones face down. The cheese is the entertainment. So is Marcel, if he's had two glasses.
  3. Stir in a figure eight. Clockwise stirrers will be gently re-educated.
  4. The crust at the bottom — la religieuse — is shared by whoever stayed to help stack chairs.
  5. No double-dipping unless everyone at the table agrees to become a family.
