Resilient habitats we track

Each zone tells a different story of tolerance — to heat, to air, to the crush of waves.

Anemone gardens

Clonal mats of Anthopleura that close against the sun and reopen with the flood tide. We track colony edges to measure heat stress.

Mid-intertidal · 112 quadrats

Mussel beds

Living breakwaters. Bed thickness buffers dozens of understory species; a single lost winter storm can reset a decade of growth.

Wave-exposed · 9 transects

Surfgrass meadows

Nursery ribbon for juvenile fish and octopus. Blade density is our earliest signal of a cooling or warming year.

Low-intertidal · 5 meadows

Sculpted pools

Granite basins that trap seawater twice a day — natural mesocosms where we watch temperature and pH swing in miniature.

Station lead · Invertebrate ecology
