Thesis

Software ate the world. Someone still has to feed the world back.

01Atoms are the frontier

The unglamorous economy — foundries, grids, ports, farms, kilns — runs on tools older than the founders replacing them. We fund the replacements.

02Founders who calibrate

We back people who have held the instrument, walked the plant floor, and can tell you the failure mode from the smell. Domain scar tissue beats pitch polish.

03Unreasonable, on purpose

Every company here was called impossible in its first hundred meetings. Reasonable plans get funded everywhere else. We keep our checkbook for the other kind.

Method

How the partnership works

First checks of $250k–$1.5m

We lead or co-lead at pre-seed and seed, usually before there is revenue and sometimes before there is a second prototype. We reserve heavily for the ugly middle years hardware always has.

A bench, not a board seat

The partnership includes a machinist, a former plant manager, and a certification engineer on retainer. When your problem is a torque spec or a UL filing, you call people who have shipped through it.

Decisions in ten days

One memo, one working session at your bench or ours, one partnership vote. We say no quickly and in writing, with the actual reason.

Patient by construction

Our fund is structured on a fourteen-year horizon because factories are not SaaS. No one here will ask you to become a marketplace.

Pitch the partnership

Send us the thing everyone said was too hard.

No warm introduction required. Write to pitch@fellowship.fund with three things: what physical problem you are ending, why you are the wrong person to talk out of it, and a photo of the earliest prototype — the uglier, the better.

  • Read by partners. Every note is read by an investing partner within one week, not screened by software.
  • Stage-honest. Napkins, garage rigs, and lab benches are all fair game. Traction is welcome, never required.
  • Anywhere with gravity. We invest wherever the physical world needs fixing, and travel to see it running.
