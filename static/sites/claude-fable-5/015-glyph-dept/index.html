03 House principles

What we believe, in the order we shout it.

  1. Letters are performers.

    A glyph that behaves identically at 12 px and 120 px is asleep on the job. Every family ships with optical cuts and variable axes so the letters act their size.

  2. Kerning is a contract.

    Each release carries hand-set pairs into the thousands — including the punctuation nobody tests until launch night.

  3. Screens first, stone second.

    We hint for low-DPI panels before we admire the specimen print. If it fails on a bus-stop display, it fails.

  4. No orphaned scripts.

    Extended Latin ships on day one, with Cyrillic and Greek in the roadmap for every text family — not as an afterthought edition.
