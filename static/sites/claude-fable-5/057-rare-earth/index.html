Nothing new from the ground

Our materials

Every gram of metal in the studio has lived a previous life. Every stone arrives with its history attached.

Recycled gold & silver

Refined from reclaimed jewelry, electronics, and mill scrap. Assayed to the same purity as newly mined metal — 18k and sterling, indistinguishable except in conscience.

Traceable stones

Sapphires from Montana, emeralds from a single Zambian cooperative, salt-and-pepper diamonds from Canadian ore. Each stone ships with its mine, cutter, and date of purchase.

Honest finishes

No plating, no lacquer, no filler. Surfaces are burnished or left raw from the hammer, so a piece wears in rather than wearing out.

From ore to object

Provenance, plainly stated

  1. 01

    Sourced

    Metal reclaimed and refined; stones purchased directly from named mines and cutters we visit.

  2. 02

    Formed

    Cast, hammered, and compressed in our Lisbon atelier. The first accident of the metal decides the form.

  3. 03

    Recorded

    Every piece is stamped with a batch mark and leaves with a printed ledger of its materials' origins.

  4. 04

    Returned

    Bring any Rare Earth piece back, ever, and we will melt it into something new — or into someone else's beginning.
