01

Open play

Court time without ceremony. Book in fifty-five-minute blocks, walk on when the light board shows green, or join the evening round-robin ladder.

  • Book & play

    Members reserve up to seven days out; guests, forty-eight hours. Balls, loaner racquets, and towels live courtside — arrive with nothing but shoes.

    55-min blocks · 06:00–23:00

  • The ladder

    A club-wide singles and doubles ladder reshuffles every Sunday night. Play one challenge match a week and the algorithm finds you an even game.

    Weekly matches · all levels

  • Machine bay

    Court Six is a dedicated ball-machine bay with programmable drills and a rear camera that plays your last rally back on the baseline monitor.

    Solo sessions · 30 min

02

Coaching

Eight resident pros, one shared philosophy: build a game you can keep for forty years. Technique first, ego last.

  • First Serves

    A six-week introduction for adults who never got the childhood lessons. Grip, rally, serve, and your first ladder match by week six.

    Cohorts monthly · 6 weeks

  • Private & semi-private

    One-on-one or two-on-one sessions with the pro of your choice, filmed on request and annotated in your locker-room inbox by morning.

    60 min · book any pro

  • Junior academy

    After-school pathways for ages 6–17, from red-ball rallies to tournament travel squads. Homework tables and dinner at The Table included.

    Weekday afternoons

  • Match play clinics

    Ninety minutes of live points with a pro on court calling patterns between games. The fastest way off a plateau.

    Tue & Thu evenings

03

Recovery

The third set shouldn't cost you the week. A quiet floor below the courts for putting yourself back together.

  • Cold plunge & sauna

    Two cedar saunas and a 7 °C plunge run on a contrast circuit. Phones stay in the locker; conversation stays at a murmur.

    Included with play

  • Bodywork studio

    Sports massage and assisted stretching from practitioners who treat tennis shoulders and tennis elbows all day, every day.

    By appointment

  • Mobility hours

    Twice-daily guided mobility sessions built around the demands of the serve. Twenty minutes, mats provided, no heroics.

    07:30 & 18:30 daily
