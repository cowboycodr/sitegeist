Why the fire

Everything starts at the hearth

Paloma began as Sunday lunches in a Roma Norte courtyard: a borrowed grill, fish from the market at La Viga, and more friends than chairs. The restaurant kept the habit. One open hearth burns encino oak from Hidalgo all night, and nearly everything you eat passes over it — snapper, sweet potatoes, even the chocolate.

We cook the coast because it's what we miss, and we finish everything with citrus because smoke asks for brightness. Come early for the quiet hour, or late, when the room is loud and the coals are perfect.
