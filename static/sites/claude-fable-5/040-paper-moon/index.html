Join the house

A ticket that lasts all year

Membership keeps the projectors threaded and the seats reupholstered. Members pay $7 at every screening, get first pick of sold-out restorations, and receive the monthly program by post — typeset, folded, and stamped by hand.

  • $7 admission to every show, every strand
  • Priority seats for restorations and one-night prints
  • The printed monthly program, mailed to your door
  • Your name in the year-end thank-you reel

$60 a year — sign up at the box office before any screening. It takes about ninety seconds.

Step-free entry, two wheelchair positions in the Main House, assistive-listening headsets at the counter, and open-caption screenings every Sunday matinee.

House rules

Phones dark, whispers only, and stay through the credits — the lights come up slowly on purpose.
