The science

Three kingdoms, one therapy

We borrow repair strategies that animals, fungi, and plants perfected over a billion years, then teach them to work together inside the human body.

Cellular

Autologous stem lines are coaxed back to a pluripotent state, then guided down a chosen lineage with light and mineral cues instead of harsh reprogramming.

Mycelial

Fungal networks form self-assembling scaffolds — breathable, biodegradable lattices that route nutrients and signal exactly where new tissue should knit.

Botanical

Plant signaling molecules — the ones that let a severed stem regrow a whole organism — become gentle instructions for human cells to remember how to heal.

The platform

Cultivar — our living foundry

Every therapy grows through the same four stages. Follow one from a single cell to a ready dose.

  1. A patient's own cells are sampled and gently returned to a stem state inside a nutrient bath tuned to their biology.

  2. Mycelial lattices are grown to the exact shape of the tissue being repaired, then seeded with the reawakened cells.

  3. Botanical growth signals orchestrate differentiation, telling each cell what to become and precisely when.

  4. The matured graft is verified, the fungal scaffold dissolves safely, and living tissue is ready for the clinic.

Our principles

Grown with intention

  • The patient is the source

    Therapies begin and end with a person's own biology — no foreign lines, minimal rejection.

  • Nothing wasted

    Every scaffold biodegrades, every process is designed to return more than it takes.

  • Slow where it matters

    We move at the speed of trust and evidence, not the speed of a press release.
