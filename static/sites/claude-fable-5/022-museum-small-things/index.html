Why small things?

Great museums keep crowns and cannons. We keep the bus ticket, the spare key, the spool of thread — objects with no market value and enormous private weight.

Each acquisition is donated with its story. Together they form an archive of ordinary attachment: how people fall in love, grieve, move house, and remember.
