What they do

Capabilities

Every cell we ship is measured against four disciplines of useful movement.

Dexterous handling

Force-guided insertion, deformable-object grasping, and bin picking under changing light. Motion plans re-solve mid-path when the part isn't where the drawing said.

Adaptive tooling

One wrist, many hands. Machines change their own tools between tasks and verify grip before committing, so a fixture change is a schedule entry, not a shutdown.

Legible motion

Trajectories are shaped so people can predict them: slow into shared space, decisive in the cell, always turning toward where they intend to go next.

Fleet coordination

Arms, carts, and tooling share one task graph. Work moves to whichever machine is free, and a stalled station reroutes around itself in seconds.

Working beside people

Safer work, by design

Safety isn't a fence around the machine. It's the machine's first constraint, solved before speed.

  • 01

    Contact-limited force

    Every joint is torque-limited below injury thresholds, and every surface a person can reach is rounded and padded.

  • 02

    Speed by proximity

    Machines scale velocity with the distance to the nearest person — full speed alone, deliberate at arm's length, still on contact.

  • 03

    Honest state

    Light rings and posture show what the machine will do next. There is no silent mode and no surprise motion.
