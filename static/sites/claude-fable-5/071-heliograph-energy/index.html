How a commission unfolds

From first light study to first kilowatt

A Heliograph project moves through four deliberate stages. You will always know which one you are in, what it costs, and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Light survey

    We model a full year of sun across your site — shadows, seasons, and weather — before a single panel is proposed.

  2. 02

    Composition

    Array geometry, frame finish, and cable routing are drawn alongside your architecture, then reviewed with you at full scale.

  3. 03

    Installation

    Certified crews build in tidy, scheduled phases. Roofs stay watertight, grounds stay open, and inspections are booked for you.

  4. 04

    Stewardship

    Performance is monitored on-site, panels are kept clean and calibrated, and a twenty-five-year yield guarantee stands behind the work.
