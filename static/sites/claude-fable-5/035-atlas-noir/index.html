The house rules

Impeccable restraint, practised daily.

  1. I

    Rare access, quietly held

    Permissions negotiated over years, never advertised. If a place could bear a crowd, we would not take you there.

  2. II

    No itinerary survives contact with wonder

    Every plan carries deliberate blank pages. When the light turns or the ice sings, the schedule yields.

  3. III

    Luxury is subtraction

    No branded amenities, no performance of service. Linen, firewood, dark sky, and someone who knows the terrain by heart.

  4. IV

    Leave the map cleaner

    Camps struck without trace, guides paid as partners, and a share of every fee returned to the landscapes that host us.

Field journal

Notes from the last charted positions.

19 February 2026 · 66°04′ N, 22°39′ W

Three hours of green

The forecast promised nothing; the sky disagreed. The party stood on the lighthouse gallery from ten until one, coats over pyjamas, and nobody said a word worth recording. Correct.

11 June 2026 · 19°17′ S, 12°34′ E

The dune that roars

Slid a metre of sand and the whole slope hummed like a cello. Our geologist explained the physics at dinner; the guests asked him to stop so they could keep believing it was the desert speaking.

27 November 2025 · 20°34′ N, 102°37′ E

Upstream, at walking pace

The boatman cut the engine below the karst and we drifted the last kilometre in silence. The abbot was waiting on the steps with tea. Some access cannot be bought — only earned, slowly.
