How it happens

The long way, on purpose

No shortcuts, no pasteurizer, no rush. Just salt, vegetables, and microbes doing what they've done forever.

  1. 01

    Chop & salt

    Seasonal produce from Hudson Valley farms meets sea salt. That's the whole ingredient list, mostly.

  2. 02

    Pack & wait

    Everything goes under brine in crocks and jars. Then the hard part: leaving it alone.

  3. 03

    Listen & taste

    We check the bubbles daily. When a batch sings — tart, alive, a little wild — it's ready.

  4. 04

    Jar & share

    Cold-packed by hand, never heat-treated, so every culture arrives at your table still working.
