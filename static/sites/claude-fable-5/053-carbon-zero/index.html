Methodology

Numbers you can defend, step by step.

Carbon Zero follows the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard end to end. Here is exactly how a raw record becomes a reported tonne.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Activity data lands from uploads and ledger extracts. Each record keeps its original document, timestamp, and owner.

  2. 02

    Classify

    Records are mapped to GHG Protocol scopes and categories with suggested matches your team confirms — never silently applied.

  3. 03

    Calculate

    Activity × factor = emissions, with the factor's publisher, vintage, and uncertainty band pinned to the result.

  4. 04

    Certify

    Reviewers approve at line level. Locked periods are immutable; restatements create a new, fully-logged version.

Boundaries and honesty

Where primary data is missing, Carbon Zero uses spend-based estimates — and says so. Every reported figure is tagged measured, calculated, or estimated, with the estimated share shown on the cover of every report. The goal is not the smallest number. It is the truest one.
