How we work

The method

  1. 01

    Market, 4 a.m.

    New Covent Garden before sunrise. We buy what looks alive and opinionated, not what a recipe demands.

  2. 02

    The bench argument

    Every composition is built by one florist and challenged by another. If nobody objects, it isn't finished.

  3. 03

    Line before mass

    We place the gesture stems first — the reachers, the leaners — then support them. Most florists work the other way.

  4. 04

    Out the door by noon

    Cargo-bike delivery inside Zones 1–2, water source to doorstep in under an hour. Flowers hate waiting rooms.
