How Kintsu works

Named for kintsugi — the craft of repairing pottery with gold — Kintsu treats the mending itself as part of the design, not something to hide.

  1. 01

    Notice, daily

    Two-minute check-ins turn vague weather into a named feeling. Over weeks, the pattern of your days becomes something you can actually see.

  2. 02

    Bring it to therapy

    Your reflections become a private summary you can share with your therapist, so sessions start where your week actually was — not where memory guesses.

  3. 03

    Practice in between

    Skills your therapist assigns live beside your check-ins: grounding, paced breathing, thought records. Small tools, exactly when the feeling is present.

Practical tools, not homework

Everything in the toolkit takes under five minutes and works offline, because hard moments rarely wait for good Wi-Fi.

  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding

    A sensory anchor for spiraling moments: five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell, one you taste.

    For anxiety

  • Paced breathing

    A quiet visual guide for four-in, six-out breathing that settles the nervous system in about ninety seconds.

    For overwhelm

  • Thought record

    Catch the automatic thought, weigh the evidence, write the kinder and truer sentence. CBT's workhorse, made pocket-sized.

    For rumination

  • Evening unload

    Three prompts before sleep that move the day's residue from your head to the page, where it can wait until morning.

    For rest

Therapy stays at the center

Kintsu is not a replacement for professional care — it is the connective tissue around it. Your therapist sees only what you choose to share, and your daily notes remain yours alone.

“The gold in kintsugi is not decoration. It is a record of care — proof that something was worth mending.”

— The idea Kintsu is built on
  • You hold the key. Sharing a week's summary is a deliberate act, never a default.
  • Session-ready. Arrive with a picture of your week instead of reconstructing it from scratch.
  • Between-session support. Assigned practices appear in your toolkit the day after a session.
