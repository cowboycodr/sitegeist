The Station

Engineered like a spacecraft. Composed like a grand hotel.

Perigee House is a pressurised ring, 64 metres across, turning once every ninety seconds to lend its outer deck a gentle lunar-grade gravity. Every corridor is a promenade; every porthole is front-row.

The Ring Promenade

A full-circle gallery deck under a continuous strip window. Walk the whole planet in eleven minutes, coffee in hand, at one-sixth of your usual weight.

Cupola Salon

Seven fused-silica panes and a tasting menu that changes with the ground track: Pacific course over the Pacific, aurora pairing over the poles.

Zero-G Atrium

At the hub, gravity lets go entirely. Guided free-flight sessions, a floating library, and the quietest sleep pods ever certified for orbit.

Arrival Node

Dock, decompress, dress for dinner. Our crew of twelve handles the checklists; you handle the view. Transit up: ninety minutes from the pad.

Suites

Twelve rooms. One planet each.

Every suite faces nadir — straight down at Earth — through a two-metre optical-grade window. Choose your light.

Limb Suite

Terminator Suite

Aurora Suite
