Seven galleries, zero “do not touch” signs

Every exhibit is scaled for small hands and enormous imaginations. Grown-ups: yes, you may play too.

  • Ages 2–6

    Block City

    A room-sized metropolis of foam bricks, arches, and cranes. Build a tower taller than a grown-up, then knock it down with ceremony.

  • Ages 0–4

    Splash Lab

    Waterwheels, whirlpools, and boats that really float. Waterproof smocks provided; giggles are not optional.

  • Ages 4–10

    Light Loft

    Paint with flashlights, bend shadows, and walk through a rainbow made of nothing but air and mirrors.

  • Ages 3–8

    Mud & Marvel Garden

    An indoor-outdoor patch for digging, planting, and meeting the museum's very patient worms.

  • Ages 5–12

    Tinker Works

    Real tools, real wood, real safety goggles. Take apart a toaster, invent a contraption, keep what you make.

  • Ages 0–3

    The Nest

    A soft, quiet cove for crawlers and first steppers, with textures to squish and gentle things that jingle.

  • Everyone

    The Question Wall

    Two stories of children's questions, hand-written and hung like stars. “Why is the moon following me?” “Do fish get thirsty?” Add yours before you leave — our explainers answer a new one every day at noon.
