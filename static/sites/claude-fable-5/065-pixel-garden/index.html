Field notes

How we grow a game, in the order it actually happens.

  1. Plant one rule. A game begins as a single sentence that shouldn't work. "Light is money." "Weather has a rewind button." We prototype the sentence, nothing else.
  2. Water it daily. Fifteen minutes of play-testing every morning, even when the build is embarrassing. Especially when the build is embarrassing.
  3. Prune loudly. Anything that doesn't serve the rule gets cut and read aloud at Friday lunch, so we remember what the garden isn't.
  4. Harvest small. We ship worlds you can finish in an evening or a weekend, priced like a good meal, playable by an eight-year-old sitting next to their grandparent.
