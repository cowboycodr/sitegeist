05 The Atlas

Four frontlines we return to every season. Choose a region to read its standing dispatch.

Delta Parishes

−16 km of shoreline since 1990 · 3 communities relocated

The river built this land over five thousand years; the sea is unbuilding it in one lifetime. Our standing dispatch follows the last shrimping fleet, the levee board’s impossible arithmetic, and the uphill villages taking shape on borrowed ground.

Glass Coast

4 fire seasons in 5 years rated “extreme” · insurance withdrawn from 11 towns

Eucalyptus hills, tile roofs, one road out. After the Black Solstice fires, rebuilding here became a question of what a town owes its future residents. We report from the planning meetings where that question is answered in concrete and setback lines.

High Plains

Aquifer down 41 m since 1950 · center-pivot circles going brown

The Ogallala took ten thousand years to fill and eighty to half-empty. Our correspondents track the water table, the wheat futures, and the families deciding whether the next generation farms at all.

Boreal Edge

Winter roads open 34 days last year — down from 76 · thaw slumps doubling per decade

North of the treeline the ground itself is the story: runways heaving, ice cellars flooding, and a carbon store older than agriculture beginning to breathe out. Recorded with, and led by, the communities who live on it.
