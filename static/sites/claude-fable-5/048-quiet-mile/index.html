The flagship

Meet the M1

One frame, one finish, one idea: a bike so quiet and so light it disappears into the ride. Every gram and every decibel was argued over.

Whisper drive

A gearless hub motor with belt drive — no chain rattle, no gear whine. Under 29 dB at cruising speed, quieter than a library reading room.

13.8 kg, all in

Full battery, fenders, lights. Light enough for a fourth-floor walk-up, balanced enough to carry with one hand on the top tube.

90 km of city

A week of commutes on one charge. The cell pack hides inside the downtube and tops up from empty in three and a half hours.

Matte stone finish

A soft mineral grey that reads as calm, not chrome. Sage detailing marks the battery line — the only place the bike raises its voice.

Design philosophy

Elegance is subtraction

Cities are loud enough. Most electric bikes add to the noise — motors that whine, apps that ping, screens that glow. We went the other way. The M1 has no display, no speaker, and no app requirement. A single dial sets assistance; a single tone of light shows charge.

What remains is the part we love: the low hiss of tyres on wet asphalt, the swing of the frame between traffic islands, the mile that passes without you noticing. We build for that mile. The quiet one.

“The best sound an electric bike can make is the city itself.”

— Quiet Mile design principle no. 1
