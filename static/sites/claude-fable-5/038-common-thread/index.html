Trace our materials

Follow the fiber

Choose a fiber to see every hand it passed through. Distances are overland freight; no stage is anonymized or aggregated.

  1. Grown Karaca family farm, Söke Plain, Türkiye

    Rain-fed organic cotton, hand-harvested in September. Third-party certified since 2019.

  2. Ginned & spun Ege Fiber Co-op, İzmir — 96 km

    Ring-spun into 30/1 yarn; gin waste is baled for mattress felt, not landfill.

  3. Knit & dyed Marmara Knitworks, Bursa — 402 km

    Closed-loop dye house recovers 91% of process water. Naturals are left entirely undyed.

  4. Sewn Atölye Nar, İstanbul — 154 km

    A 22-person workshop paid 1.4× the regional living wage, audited annually with public results.

  1. Grown Ferme Delaunay, Normandy, France

    Flax dew-retted in the field — no water, no chemicals, just morning fog and patience.

  2. Scutched & spun Lin des Flandres, Kortrijk, Belgium — 289 km

    Long-line fibers wet-spun for strength; short tow becomes our repair thread.

  3. Woven Tissage Aubert, Lille — 28 km

    A 240 gsm plain weave on refurbished 1980s looms, left in the flax's natural oat color.

  4. Sewn Atelier Fil Rouge, Porto, Portugal — 1,520 km

    Flat-felled seams throughout, so the overshirt has no raw edge anywhere inside.

  1. Grown Flock 217, Ouse Valley, Tasmania

    Non-mulesed merino, 18.5 micron, shorn once a year by a crew the farm has used for a decade.

  2. Scoured & combed Launceston Wool Works — 84 km

    Scoured with recovered lanolin sold on to cosmetics makers; nothing skimmed is wasted.

  3. Spun & knit Bendigo Woollen Mills, Victoria — 660 km

    Two-ply yarn knit fully fashioned on Shima machines — every panel shaped, none cut.

  4. Finished Same mill, same floor — 0 km

    Linked, washed, and blocked in one building. The shortest last mile we have ever recorded.

Designed to be repaired

Mending is part of the garment

Every piece ships with a repair card, matched thread, and a promise: we will mend it for as long as you own it.

1

Spare everything

Buttons, thread, and a patch of the exact fabric lot are sewn into an inner pocket of every garment.

2

Free mending, forever

Post us a worn piece and our workshop repairs it at no charge — visibly or invisibly, your choice.

3

Take-back, not throwaway

Beyond repair? Return it for a credit. Cotton and linen are shredded for new yarn; wool is reknit.

The long-story guaranteeEvery Edition 04 garment carries a stitched-in lot number. Look it up on the card in your pocket and you will find the farm, the mill, the workshop, and the date your fiber was harvested — the whole thread, uncut.
