Why maps, not folders

Your research already has a shape. Narra lets you see it.

Sources that stay cited

Drop in papers, interviews, links, and datasets. Every excerpt keeps a live pointer back to its origin, page, and timestamp.

Notes that link themselves

As you write, Narra suggests connections to related notes and contradicting evidence — you approve them with one keystroke.

Threads that become drafts

Pull nodes onto a storyline and Narra assembles an outlined draft, evidence attached, ready for your voice.

Made for many minds

Colleagues annotate the same map in real time. Disagreements become labeled forks, not lost comments.

The Narra method

From pile to plot in three moves

  1. 01

    Gather

    Bring everything in — PDFs, transcripts, spreadsheets, stray thoughts. Nothing needs a folder yet.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Link claims to evidence and ideas to each other. The map grows as your understanding does.

  3. 03

    Narrate

    Trace a thread through the map. Narra turns the path into a structured, fully-cited draft.
