01Experiments

Seven live prototypes. Select any card to read its field notes. Statuses update whenever something breaks in an interesting way.

  • EXP-014 Live

    Peripheral Compiler

    A code editor that renders your program as weather at the edge of your vision. Errors arrive as thunderheads long before the linter notices.

    Day 41: participants report they can feel a null-pointer forming, described variously as "drizzle" and "dread." We consider this a success metric now.

  • EXP-019 Live

    Room-Scale Undo

    Spatial computing with a rewind key. Walk backwards through the last ninety seconds of a physical room, annotations and spilled coffee included.

    Day 12: the undo buffer now survives lunch. Redo remains philosophically contested and is disabled until the ethics reading group reconvenes.

  • EXP-023 Drifting

    Sympathetic Cursor

    A pointer trained on ten years of hesitation data. It pre-flinches at destructive buttons and lingers, wistfully, over things you almost clicked.

    Day 67: the cursor has begun avoiding the settings page entirely. We are choosing to interpret this as machine perception rather than a bug.

  • EXP-031 Live

    Depth-First Wallpaper

    Machine-perceived rooms re-rendered as infinitely recursive interiors. Your wall contains a smaller office, which contains a smaller you, working harder.

    Day 8: recursion capped at depth 12 after a volunteer waved at themselves for an hour. The smaller you does not wave back. We checked.

  • EXP-036 Halted

    Consensus Keyboard

    Every keypress is a vote. Text appears only when all connected typists agree on the next character. Produces one immaculate sentence per week.

    Day 90: halted after the group deadlocked for six days between "the" and "a". The sentence so far is perfect. We refuse to publish it.

  • EXP-040 Drifting

    Haptic Horizon

    A wristband that renders distant deadlines as a faint gravitational tug. Time management, reimagined as an inner-ear problem.

    Day 29: testers now lean slightly toward Thursday. Calibration continues. The control group, tragically, remains upright.

  • EXP-044 Live

    Glossolalia Shell

    A terminal that accepts commands in any language you almost know. It responds in the one you were born with, gently corrected.

    Day 55: rm now requires an apology in the imperative mood. Accidental deletions down 94%. Sincerity unverifiable.

02Manifesto

Four commitments, revised annually, broken weekly.

  1. Build the wrong thing beautifully.

    A dead end explored with care teaches more than a shortcut taken on faith. Our failures are load-bearing; we document them like victories.

  2. Interfaces should be a little strange.

    Familiarity is a sedative. A small, honest strangeness keeps the hand thinking and the eye awake. Delight is not a rounding error — it is the spec.

  3. Machines may perceive; people decide.

    We give sensors curiosity and models opinions, but the last word in every loop belongs to a human who can say NULL and mean it.

  4. Ship the question.

    A prototype is a question made touchable. If the answer were known, it would be a product, and products belong to some other, tidier building.
