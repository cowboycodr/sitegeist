The system

Five planes. One grid. Every room.

Plane Object is built on a 400 mm module. Panels, uprights, and joints interlock on that grid, so every part you own today fits every piece we make tomorrow.

  • Panel

    The horizontal plane. Shelf, seat, or desktop — 400, 800, or 1 200 mm wide.

  • Upright

    The vertical plane. Slotted every 200 mm so panels land exactly where you need them.

  • Joint

    Solid brass bolt with an oversized head. Tightens by hand, loosens by hand, lasts a lifetime.

  • Wedge

    The ash wedge that locks a panel into an upright. Tap it in with your palm; tap it out to move.

  • Frame

    Powder-coated steel back-brace for tall builds. Optional, visible, and proud of it.
