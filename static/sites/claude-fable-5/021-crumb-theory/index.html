Pastry notes

What we learned at the oven this week, written down before we forget.

Thursday, July 16

On resting rye

An extra forty minutes of autolyse turned the rye kouign-amann from stubborn to silk. The honey now goes on after the fold, not before — it was tearing the layers.

Tuesday, July 14

Canelé shell, corrected

We chased a matte shell for a month. The answer was boring: colder batter, hotter first ten minutes, and leaving the oven door shut like we tell everyone else to.

Sunday, July 12

Crumb reading

Wild, uneven holes look impressive but carry butter poorly. We are steering the sourdough toward a tighter, glossy crumb. Theory: bread should serve the toast.
