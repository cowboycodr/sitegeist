How we work

Patience is the recipe

  1. 01

    Forage at first light

    We gather sage, ocotillo, juniper, and citrus before the heat rises, taking only what the land can spare and logging every plot we touch.

  2. 02

    Macerate through the heat

    Botanicals rest in neutral spirit through the hottest hours. The desert afternoon does the extraction; we just keep notes and taste often.

  3. 03

    Distill at the edge of daylight

    The still runs in the evening, when the air cools and the cuts come clean. Every batch is small enough to be a single long shadow of work.
