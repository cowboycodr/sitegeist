Inside every case

Calibre MW-01

Designed on paper before it was designed in software, the MW-01 is a hand-wound movement with a free-sprung balance and a stop-seconds hack. Bridges are German silver, bevelled and black-polished by hand; the ratchet wheel carries a solarised sunburst you will only ever see through the caseback.

Enduring materials

Chosen to outlast their owner

Nothing on a Meridian is plated, veneered, or provisional. Every material is selected so the watch can be serviced — not replaced — for a century.

  • 904L steel

    Case and buckle are milled from 904L bar stock: harder to machine than 316L, warmer in colour, and far more resistant to the acids in daily wear.

  • Box sapphire

    A domed box-section sapphire crystal, anti-reflective on the inner face only, so the dial reads black-on-cream in any light without a blue sheen.

  • German silver

    Movement bridges in untreated maillechort. It tarnishes to a soft champagne over decades — the movement ages alongside the person wearing it.

  • Grand Feu enamel

    Dials fired repeatedly at 820 °C until the surface is glass. One dial in three survives the kiln; the failures are ground back and refired.

Patient assembly

Nine weeks, two hands

One watchmaker follows each watch from first bridge to final regulation. This is the sequence, and it is never compressed.

  1. 01

    Component finishing

    Three weeks of anglage, black polishing, and perlage before a single part is assembled. Every screw slot is polished and re-blued in-house.

  2. 02

    First assembly

    The movement is fully built, run for ten days, then completely disassembled, cleaned, and inspected under 20× magnification.

  3. 03

    Final assembly & oiling

    Rebuilt with fresh lubricants at nine oiling points, each metered by eye through a microscope. The balance is poised and sprung by hand.

  4. 04

    Regulation & casing

    Fifteen days of timing in six positions and two temperatures. Only then is the movement cased, sealed, and signed on its certificate.
