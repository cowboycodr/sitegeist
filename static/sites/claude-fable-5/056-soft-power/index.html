About Soft Power

Soft Power is an independent culture magazine. We take the unserious seriously: the fonts, feeds, rooms, and rituals that quietly decide what feels normal. No sponsors sit in on edits, no trend is covered as a favor, and every issue is reported from the ground rather than the timeline.

— The Editors
