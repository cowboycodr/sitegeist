Method

The speed of care

From standing tree to numbered stamp, a piece takes years. We think that is the point.

  1. I

    Felled with a name

    Every tree comes from within sixty miles and is logged with where it stood and why it came down — storm, disease, or a road that needed widening.

  2. II

    Seasoned in the loft

    Boards rest in stick for a year per inch of thickness. The loft does the work; we only turn the stacks and wait.

  3. III

    Joined without screws

    Wedged tenons, drawbored mortises, sliding dovetails. Joints that tighten with the seasons instead of loosening against them.

  4. IV

    Numbered and recorded

    Each piece is stamped and entered in the ledger with its tree, its maker, and its year — so it can be repaired, and remembered, for good.

“A scratch is not damage. It is the first entry your household makes in the ledger we started.”

— The workshop note stamped inside every piece
