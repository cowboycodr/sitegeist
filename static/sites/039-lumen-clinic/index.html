Is Lumen a replacement for my primary care doctor?+

Lumen complements your primary care with a deeper preventive lens. We focus on risk, patterns, and long-range health planning while your primary doctor remains the right home for everyday care.

What happens during my first year?+

You’ll complete an intake, comprehensive diagnostics, and a 90-minute review. Your clinician then builds a prioritized roadmap and checks in throughout the year.

Can I use HSA or FSA funds?+

Many members use HSA or FSA funds for eligible services. We provide itemized documentation; confirm eligibility with your plan administrator.
