How we bake

Three folds. One quiet hour.

We mill what we can, culture our butter overnight, and laminate only when the dough is ready — never when the clock says so. Seasonal fruit is reduced low and slow so the pastry stays the loud part of the room.

  • Heritage grain Stoneground wheat, rye, and spelt from small mills.
  • Cultured butter High-fat European style, turned in-house for depth.
  • Seasonal fruit Market fruit laminated into jam, folds, and finishes.
