Explore the science

Three living systems. One regenerative path.

Nature already knows how to heal. Bloom translates cellular plasticity, fungal architecture, and plant communication into therapies designed for durable human recovery.

Cellular renewal

We guide patient-derived cells through controlled reprogramming, producing living grafts that integrate, remodel, and restore function rather than merely patching damage.

  • Autologous cell lines
  • Matrix-guided differentiation
  • Immune-compatible grafts

Fungal architecture

Mycelium is nature’s structural network. Bloom cultivates tunable fungal scaffolds that host cells, guide vascular growth, and dissolve as living tissue takes over.

  • Porous living matrices
  • Programmable degradation
  • Vascular channel patterning

Plant intelligence

Plants sense injury, redistribute resources, and regenerate organs. We adapt those signaling pathways into molecular cues that instruct human tissues to rebuild with precision.

  • Injury-response mimetics
  • Growth-factor orchestration
  • Spatially timed dosing
