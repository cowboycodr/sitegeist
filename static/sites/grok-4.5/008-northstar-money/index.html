FAQ

Straight answers

Is Northstar a robo-advisor that holds my money?

No. Northstar is guidance software. Your assets stay at the brokers and banks you choose. We help you plan, allocate, and stay on course.

How accurate are the projections?

Projections use transparent compound-growth assumptions you control. They are educational estimates, not guarantees of future performance. Markets vary; your plan should too.

Can I use Northstar without linking accounts?

Yes. Enter balances manually on any plan. Linking is optional on True North and Guided for automatic refreshes and fee detection.

Do you sell my financial data?

Never. We do not sell portfolio data or personal information to advertisers. Connections are read-only where supported.

Who is Northstar built for?

People who want long-term wealth building with clear trade-offs—especially dual-income households, early-career savers, and anyone tired of spreadsheet sprawl.
