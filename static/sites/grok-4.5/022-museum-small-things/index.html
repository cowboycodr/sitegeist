Philosophy

We collect what museums usually overlook

Grand halls favor crowns and canvases. We favor the lint of living: the object that looks worthless until you hear why it survived every clean-out.

Stories are accessioned with the same care as materials. Provenance may be a kitchen drawer. Significance may be a single afternoon. Both are enough.

  1. 01
    Small scale, full dignity No object is too minor if a person still carries its meaning.
  2. 02
    Story first, spectacle never We present things quietly so their histories can speak without noise.
  3. 03
    Consent and care Contributors control how much of a life is shown beside an object.

Themes

How ordinary things gather meaning

Four recurring paths through the collection—useful for browsing, not for boxing anyone in.

Leaving

Keys, tickets, cards, and fragments kept after rooms, cities, and versions of the self were closed.

Love

Tokens of care that never needed to be expensive—only durable enough to outlast the occasion.

Childhood

Playthings and papers that still smell, somehow, like summer and fluorescent classrooms.
