Noise is not a feature of city life. It is a design failure.

Quiet Mile builds electric bikes that lower the volume of getting around — light enough to carry up stairs, silent enough to share sidewalks kindly, and refined enough to feel like part of your day rather than another machine in it.

Designed for density

Movement without the friction.

Every detail serves calm progress: fewer rattles, fewer stops for charging anxiety, and geometry that reads as furniture when you lean it in a hallway.

Near-silent assist

A rear hub motor with sealed internals and a carbon-reinforced belt keep the soundtrack to wind, tires, and the street itself.

Carry-up light

A monocoque alloy frame and integrated 320 Wh pack bring the M1 to 14.2 kg — light enough for brownstone stairs and loft elevators.

City-true range

Tuned for stop-and-go streets, not brochure hills. Expect about 72 km of mixed urban riding with assist that never shouts for attention.

Low-maintenance belt

No chain lube on your trousers. The carbon belt runs clean for years and pairs with an internal gear hub for weatherproof simplicity.

Everyday security

Frame lock mount, hidden battery release, and a discreet GPS slot keep the M1 present when you step away for coffee or a meeting.

Soft geometry

Upright posture, swept bars, and a step-through option mean you arrive composed — not hunched, not hurried, not in athletic gear.

From door to dusk

A quieter mile, every day.

Ownership is meant to feel unremarkable in the best way — charge overnight, roll out, lock, and forget the machine until morning.

  1. Wake charged

    The pack tops up from a standard outlet in under three hours. Status glows softly — never a loud LED parade.

  2. Roll into the grid

    Assist engages with a long, quiet ramp. You choose the cadence; the motor fills the gaps between traffic lights.

  3. Arrive composed

    No chain grease, no overheating, no dramatic power cuts. Just the soft click of the frame lock and a calmer arrival.
