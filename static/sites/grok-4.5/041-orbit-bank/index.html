FAQ

Questions, answered cleanly

Is Orbit a real bank?

Orbit partners with regulated banking institutions. Cash balances are held at partner banks and eligible deposits are FDIC insured up to applicable limits. Investing services are provided through an affiliated broker-dealer.

Can I use Orbit in more than one country?

Yes. Hold multiple currencies, receive local account details where available, and spend abroad with real-time conversion. Availability of local details varies by corridor and plan.

How do savings rates work?

Vault APY is variable and published in-app. Rate shown on this page is illustrative. Interest accrues daily on eligible balances and is paid monthly into your vault.

What does investing cost?

Self-directed trades on Path and Horizon have $0 commissions on stocks and ETFs. Orbit Path managed portfolios charge a clear annual advisory fee disclosed before you enroll. Market spreads and fund expense ratios still apply.

How do I close or export my data?

You can download statements and tax documents anytime. To close your account, contact support from Settings—we’ll help you move remaining balances and cancel cards cleanly.
