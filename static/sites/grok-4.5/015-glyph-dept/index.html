The desk

A small department with loud letters

Glyph Department is an independent type foundry. We draw variable faces that stretch across viewports, collide in display lockups, and perform under motion without losing readability.

Our process pairs traditional spacing craft with screen-first testing. Every release ships with specimen notes, licensing that respects independent studios, and axes you can actually use.

  • Variable-first releases for real product systems
  • Optical sizes for UI and editorial pairings
  • Licenses for freelancers, studios, and products

Principles

  1. 01 Voice before novelty. A glyph is only useful if it says something.
  2. 02 Axes with purpose. Width, weight, and optical size must earn their place.
  3. 03 Screen is the stage. If it fails at 390px, it fails.
  4. 04 Independent by design. Built by a tight team, licensed without theater.
