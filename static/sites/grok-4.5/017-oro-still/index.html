Our materials

Metal that holds time

We work only with metals chosen for how they age on the body: gold that warms, silver that softens, alloys mixed in-house so every surface feels intentional under the thumb.

  • 22k & 18k gold Recycled Italian gold, cast in small pours for a dense, living grain.
  • Sterling silver Hand-polished to a soft satin—never mirror-bright, never cold.
  • Rose alloys Copper-rich blends that deepen with wear into a private patina.
  • Lost-wax casting Each form begins as wax carved by hand, then burned out and filled with molten metal.

Cast, cooled, and finished in Florence

The atelier

Stillness as a method

“We make objects slow enough to become personal—pieces you forget you are wearing until someone asks about the story.”

Oro Still began as a single bench in a courtyard workshop off Via dei Servi. The name is deliberate: gold that is still—unhurried, held, complete.

There is no catalogue of seasons. When a form is ready, it enters a small edition. When that edition is finished, the mold is retired. What remains is yours alone.
