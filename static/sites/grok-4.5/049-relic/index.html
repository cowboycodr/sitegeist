Our standard

Weathered is not the same as worn out

Relic exists for people who prefer objects with a past—and sellers who document that past carefully. We favor repair, provenance notes, and materials that improve with touch.

  • 01
    Material honesty

    Wood, metal, clay, wool. We refuse plastic-clad “vintage style” reproductions.

  • 02
    Repairable structure

    If it cannot be maintained with ordinary tools, it does not enter the market.

  • 03
    Clear condition notes

    Scratches, patches, and replaced parts are listed—never airbrushed out of photos.

“A good object should outlast the room it sits in—and leave a better story than it found.”

— Relic buying desk
