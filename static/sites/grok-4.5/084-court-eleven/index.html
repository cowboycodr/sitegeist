01 — Open play

Show up. Find a game. Stay late.

Court Eleven runs open play like a well-kept house—rotating partners, clear etiquette, and enough room for first-timers and former college players to share a net without ceremony.

Morning drop-in

Weekdays 06:00–10:00. Self-organized sets with a host desk that pairs levels in under five minutes.

Members free · Guests $28

Lunch ladder

Tuesdays & Thursdays, 11:30–14:00. Quick doubles, standing ladders, and a reserved courtside table for the post-set salad.

Advance sign-up

Night court

Fridays from 19:00. Dimmed house lights, clay and hard under the same roof, music kept low enough to hear the bounce.

Social open play

02 — Coaching

Technique with a city pace.

Our coaches work in short, focused blocks: footwork clinics, serve studios, match-play labs. No multi-hour lectures—just clear cues you can take onto the next open set.

  • Private sessions on hard or clay
  • Small-group clinics (max 4)
  • Junior after-school pathway
  • Video review in the gallery loft
Reserve a lesson
11

Lessons book in 30- and 55-minute blocks. Coaches rotate across open play so progress shows up in real games, not only drills.

03 — Recovery

The other half of the rally.

Downstairs from the courts: cold plunge, contrast showers, quiet stretching room, and a recovery desk staffed by trainers who understand sore shoulders and tight hips.

Contrast circuit

Hot–cold sequence with timed intervals and guided breath. Members book 45-minute slots after play.

Movement room

Mats, bands, and a short menu of post-match mobility. Open access all day for members.

Bodywork desk

Sports massage and soft-tissue work by appointment—same building, no street-level scramble.

04 — Hospitality

All-day house, not a locker-room café.

The House is Court Eleven’s ground-floor room: coffee from six, lunch until late afternoon, wine and small plates after evening sets. Stay in club whites or change upstairs—the same tables host both.

Morning
Espresso, soft eggs, toast with house jam
Midday
Grain bowls, grilled fish, seasonal salads
Evening
Natural wine list, cheese boards, late pasta

“We built the bar so the match conversation doesn’t have to end at the baseline.”

— House note, opening season
