The industrial reality

Most heat never becomes work.

Furnaces, kilns, data centers, and process lines reject enormous thermal energy into air and water. Thermal closes that loop—capturing residual heat before it escapes and converting it into electricity and process steam.

Without recovery

Energy leaves as exhaust

High-grade and low-grade heat stream out of stacks, cooling towers, and radiator banks. Plants pay twice: once for fuel, again for cooling infrastructure that only discards value.

With Thermal

Heat becomes baseload power

Modular recovery units sit on existing exhaust paths. Organic Rankine cycles, heat pumps, and thermal storage turn waste streams into dispatchable, carbon-free energy on site.

Platform

Systems built for real plants

Engineered for continuous duty in harsh industrial environments—retrofit-friendly, metered end-to-end, and sized from single lines to multi-megawatt campuses.

Capture modules

Compact heat exchangers mate to flue gas, hot water, and process oil loops without interrupting production. Alloy stacks handle 80–450°C residual streams.

Conversion core

Organic Rankine and supercritical CO₂ cycles spin generators from recovered heat. Smart controls track load, temperature, and grid export in real time.

Thermal storage

Phase-change and sensible storage banks smooth intermittent process heat so recovery stays productive through shift changes, batch cycles, and weekend turndowns.

Deployment

From waste stream to watt-hours

A clear path from audit to continuous generation—engineered to install beside live operations.

  1. Map the heat

    Instrument exhaust, cooling loops, and process returns to quantify recoverable megawatts.

  2. Size the loop

    Match capture hardware and cycle chemistry to temperature bands and duty cycles.

  3. Integrate cleanly

    Install on bypass ducts and return headers with fail-safe isolation for plant safety.

  4. Deliver power

    Feed site loads or export—metered, dispatchable, and reported against carbon targets.
