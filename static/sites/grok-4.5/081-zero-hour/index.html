How Zero Hour works

01 — Investigations

Follow the system, not the press release

We dig into budgets, models, and chain-of-command decisions that decide who floods, who bakes, and who gets rebuilt last.

02 — Local voices

Primary sources before punditry

Residents, workers, and keepers of local memory are not color for a story— they are the record. We quote them in full light.

03 — Visual evidence

Show the threshold, don’t narrate it away

Photographs, stills, and measured maps sit beside the text so distance cannot soften what is already happening.
