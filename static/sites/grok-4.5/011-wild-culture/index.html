How we work

Four slow steps

  1. 01

    Source seasonally

    We buy what the market is heavy with—cabbage in fall, peppers in late summer, citrus when winter needs brightness.

  2. 02

    Salt & seal

    Vegetables meet salt, weight, and glass. No vinegar shortcuts. Just the right salinity for wild lactic cultures to thrive.

  3. 03

    Wait (and taste)

    Batches rest in our climate-controlled culture room. We taste daily, note bubbles, and only bottle when the flavor says go.

  4. 04

    Share live

    Jars leave cold and alive. Keep them refrigerated, eat with gusto, and return empties for a refill credit.
