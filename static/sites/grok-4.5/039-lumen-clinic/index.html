FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Still deciding? Here is what members ask before their first panel.

Do I need a referral?

No. Membership includes ordering and interpretation. If something needs specialist follow-up, we prepare a clear summary you can share with your primary clinician.

Where do I get my blood drawn?

We partner with national labs and select clinic suites. After you join, you choose a location online and book a time that fits your week.

How is this different from a yearly physical?

Annual physicals are essential. Lumen adds deeper biomarker coverage, longitudinal tracking, and a preventive plan focused on trends—not only today’s abnormal flags.

Is my data private?

Yes. Results are encrypted at rest and in transit, shared only with your assigned care team, and never sold. You can request export or deletion of membership records at any time.
