Clinical notes

Formulas written for the barrier.

Every Aera product is screened for fragrance, essential oils, and high-irritant denatured alcohols. We publish what matters—and leave out what doesn’t.

  • Ceramide complex

    NP, AP, and EOP ceramides mirrored to the skin’s own lipid matrix—supporting cohesion where dryness begins.

  • Multi-weight HA

    High- and low-molecular hyaluronic acid for surface slip and deeper hydration without a sticky film.

  • Niacinamide 4%

    A measured dose to refine tone and reinforce barrier enzymes—calm enough for reactive routines.

Patch-tested on sensitive skin panels. Dermatologist-reviewed formulations. Not tested on animals. Made in small batches for freshness, not scarcity theater.
