The kiln method

Patient heat. Exact profiles.

A kiln is a vessel of controlled fire. We roast the same way — measuring every degree, listening to first crack, and recording what the coffee wants rather than forcing a formula.

  1. 01

    Source with intention

    Direct relationships with producers who mill carefully and harvest for quality over volume.

  2. 02

    Sample & cup

    Green evaluation and cupping across multiple roast levels before a profile is locked.

  3. 03

    Profile the fire

    Charge temperature, rate of rise, and development time tuned lot by lot on drum roasters.

  4. 04

    Rest & release

    Degassing windows observed so flavor opens fully when the bag reaches your kitchen.
