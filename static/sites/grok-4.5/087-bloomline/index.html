Point of view

Less bouquet. More sculpture.

Bloomline treats flowers as architecture—line, weight, negative space—so a composition holds its own long after the first glance.

“We design for the pause: the moment a room exhales.”

Based in London, we work with seasonal British growers and a short list of European specialists when a form demands it.

  • 01

    Form first

    Stems are chosen for gesture and silhouette, then colour. Symmetry is optional; intention is not.

  • 02

    Season as brief

    Spring means acid greens and tight buds. Autumn means smoke, berry, and copper. We never force a stem out of season.

  • 03

    Rooms, not recipes

    Installations respond to light, ceiling height, and how people move—homes, galleries, dinners, and yes, ordinary Tuesdays.

How we work

From brief to bloom

A clear path whether you need a single gift stem or a room that stops people mid-step.

1

Share the room

Occasion, palette leanings, vessel preference, and any hard constraints—pets, scent sensitivity, delivery window.

2

Sketch the form

We reply with direction, stem options, and a fee range. Large installs may include a site visit.

3

Source & build

Market morning, studio composition, then careful packing or on-site installation by the team.

4

Arrive & settle

Delivery across London with care notes. Installations include placement, hydration, and a quiet tidy-up.
