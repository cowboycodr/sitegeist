Meet the studio

Four people, one shared plot

We make games the way gardeners keep a greenhouse: carefully, seasonally, and a little stubborn about pests. Pixel Garden started as a weekend jam table and grew into a remote studio across three time zones.

Our rule of thumb: if a mechanic can’t be explained with a crayon sketch, it isn’t ready to plant.

  • Playable first Vertical slices before mood boards get precious.
  • Readable pixels Clarity at a glance, atmosphere on a second look.
  • Strange, not cruel Weird systems that invite curiosity instead of punishment.

From the journal

Nov 12, 2025

Why Moss Circuit flips the map at night

We wanted exploration that feels like a second game without doubling content. When the canopy rewrites paths, players reuse landmarks as if the forest itself is joking with them.

Aug 3, 2025

Controller glyphs that kids can read

Prompt icons stay chunky and high-contrast. Color is backup, not the message—because shared couches and sunny rooms are part of our design brief.

Mar 21, 2025

Shipping small on purpose

A two-to-six hour world is a promise: we will not pad. If a side quest only exists to inflate a checklist, it stays in the compost bin.
