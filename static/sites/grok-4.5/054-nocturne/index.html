Fragrance for the hours after midnight

Nocturne is a house of night scents — compositions that open slowly, speak in low registers, and leave a cool trail of ash, petal, and rain. Each perfume is built for skin that has cooled, rooms that have emptied, and the thin light before dawn.

  • Smoke Incense, burned wood, the last curl of a candle
  • Mineral Wet stone, metal, salt air after a storm
  • Night flower Jasmine, tuberose, and blooms that open only after dusk
  • Weather Distant rain, cold wind, the charge before lightning

Philosophy

Composed in low light

We blend for the skin after the day has left it — cooler, quieter, more honest. Nocturne scents avoid bright citrus glare and heavy gourmand sweetness. Instead they favor incense smoke, mineral coolness, white flowers that only open at night, and the smell of weather moving in from far away.

Every formula is finished by hand in small batches. Concentration sits between eau de parfum and extrait so a single wearing lasts from evening into the pale hours of morning.

“Night is not the absence of light. It is another kind of weather.”

— House Nocturne
  • No daylight florals
  • No synthetic sugar
  • Mineral bases over musk alone
  • Smoke as structure, not gimmick

Wearing ritual

How to wear the night

  1. 01

    Cool skin

    Apply after the day has left your pulse points cool — wrists, collarbones, the nape of the neck.

  2. 02

    Two places only

    Nocturne is concentrated. Two careful touches are enough for a full evening into morning.

  3. 03

    Let it open

    Give smoke and mineral ten minutes to settle before judging the dry-down. Night scents bloom slowly.
