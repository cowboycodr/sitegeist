Ground

Place

Joshua Tree high desert

Forty minutes from the park entrance, on private land with open sightlines and dark skies. Bring layers — desert nights drop fast.

  • When September 18–20 · gates daily from mid-afternoon
  • Where Solar Flare grounds · Joshua Tree, California
  • Getting there Shuttle from Yucca Valley · rideshare drop zone · limited on-site parking
  • Leave no trace Pack out what you pack in · compost & recycle stations on every path

Desert kit

  • Headlamp (red mode preferred)
  • Dust scarf & closed shoes
  • Refillable bottle (1L+)
  • Warm layer for after midnight
  • Sunscreen for the golden hour
