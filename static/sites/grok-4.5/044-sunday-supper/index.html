Our table

A room built for long evenings

Sunday Supper began as a weekly open-door dinner among friends. Now the door stays open all week — same generosity, same pace.

We cook what the market and nearby farms offer each morning: big salads, slow roasts, bread still warm from the oven, and desserts that feel like someone’s grandmother still lives nearby.

There is no rush to turn tables. Order a bottle, order another course, stay for one more story. Supper is the point.

By the glass & bottle

Natural wine, chosen for supper

Low-intervention bottles from growers we trust — bright, earthy, and generous enough for a long night at the table.

Loire

Chenin on the cooler side

Honeyed orchard fruit, soft acid, perfect with roast chicken and early courses.

Beaujolais

Gamay for the whole table

Juicy red berries, gentle tannin, and the kind of pour people keep reaching for.

Sicily

Volcanic whites

Saline, citrus-driven bottles that love tomatoes, anchovy, and wood-roasted fish.
