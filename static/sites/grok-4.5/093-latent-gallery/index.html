Algorithmic editions

Three states of the show

Each panel is a self-contained generative practice. Interact to reseed; motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

02

Orbital Ribbons

Closed curves braided by phased oscillators. Each reseed rearranges period and phase offset.

03

Bloom Lattice

A soft cellular automaton that expands and erodes. Growth rules stay constant; the seed does not.

04

Interference Veil

Living systems

How the gallery thinks

01

Seeded, not stored

Works begin from a seed at runtime. There is no master file and no permanent raster. What you see is the system, not a recording of it.

02

Machine-assisted craft

Artists author rules, palettes, and constraints. Algorithms carry those intentions into states no single hand could enumerate.

03

Local, continuous runtime

Everything runs in your browser. No remote models, no tracking, no network calls after load — only local computation and light.

04

Motion with consent

Living pieces respect reduced-motion preferences, pause on demand, and remain legible as still compositions when animation rests.
