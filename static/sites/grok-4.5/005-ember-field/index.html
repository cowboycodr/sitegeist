From ridge to roast

Coffee that keeps the altitude

Ember & Field traces seasonal lots from highland farms where mornings start in mist and harvests follow the rain, not a calendar. We buy ripe cherry from small producers, dry it where it was grown, and roast in short batches until the cup tastes like the place.

No endless menus. No warehouse blend designed for sameness. Each release is finite— named for the field, finished over slow heat, and meant to be remembered long after the bag is empty.

“We roast for clarity and warmth: the field first, then the ember.”

How a lot becomes a cup

The field-to-ember ritual

Four deliberate steps. No shortcuts that erase place. No roast profile that flattens the harvest.

  1. Pick ripe

    Only deep-red cherry, hand-sorted on the slope the morning it is picked.

  2. Dry in place

    Raised beds and patios beside the field so the weather of the ridge stays in the seed.

  3. Roast slow

    Small drum loads, extended development, cooled before the sweetness turns to ash.

  4. Cup & name

    We taste, name the field, bag within days, and ship while the roast still sings.
