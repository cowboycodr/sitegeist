About the club

A supper club that moves. A pot that doesn’t.

Fondue Club is a ticketed, roving melt — living rooms, gallery backrooms, and the occasional greenhouse. We haul the burners, shred the alpine cheeses, chill the white wine, and hand you a fork with zero judgment.

Evenings run long, conversations run longer, and nobody leaves hungry. Membership is informal: if you show up, you belong. If you double-dip, you’re family.

  • Small rooms, long tables, 18 seats max
  • Seasonal host kitchens across the city
  • All-you-can-dip, until the pot says otherwise
  • Vegetarian-friendly pots every night; vegan on request

The menu

What goes in the pot (and what you dunk)

Three rotating pots, a bread basket that never ends, and wine chosen for melt-compatibility.

Alpine Three-Cheese

house pot

Gruyère, emmental, and comté melted with dry white wine, garlic, and a whisper of nutmeg. The classic for a reason.

Midnight Raclette Blend

rotating

Raclette and fontina with black pepper and pickled shallot oil. Louder, saltier, slightly dangerous after midnight.

Herb Garden Tomme

green night

Soft tomme with chive, tarragon, and lemon zest. Lighter melt for summer host kitchens and greenhouse tables.

Crusty sourdough cubes

endless

Day-old loaves torn and toasted. The official vehicle of the club.

Baby potatoes & cornichons

side

Boiled new potatoes, sharp pickles, and blistered cherry tomatoes for acid and crunch.

Charred broccoli & apples

side

Because greens count when they’re dragged through cheese. Crisp apple for the brave.

Alpine white pour

included

Crisp, cold, and stubbornly drinkable. Refills encouraged; keys collected as needed.

House kirsch spritz

+4

A little cherry brandy, a lot of sparkle. Tradition with a wink.

Zero-proof pear fizz

included

Pressed pear, tonic, and thyme. For designated dippers and the wisely cautious.

House rules

Keep it molten, keep it kind

01

Stir, don’t stir drama

Figure-eight the pot so nobody gets a scorched bottom. Conversations should follow the same rule.

02

Lost bread pays a toll

Drop your cube in the pot and tradition says you buy the next round — or tell a story worth the cheese.

03

Phones face-down

Except for the group photo at dessert. We’re here for faces, forks, and the soft clink of glasses.
