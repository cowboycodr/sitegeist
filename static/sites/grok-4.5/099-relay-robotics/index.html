How a Relay run works

No new workflows to invent. Staff place a job; the building does the walking.

  1. 01

    Request

    Scan a QR, tap the app, or push from your existing EHR / PMS. Pick a cargo type and destination.

  2. 02

    Dispatch

    Fleet software assigns the nearest charged robot and plots a clear multi-floor route around live traffic.

  3. 03

    Navigate

    Relay One rides elevators, waits politely, and announces itself at the door with a soft chime and friendly face.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    Authorized unlock, digital signature, and automatic return-to-dock or next job—logged end to end.
