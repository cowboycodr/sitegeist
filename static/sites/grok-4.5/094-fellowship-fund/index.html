Thesis

Ambition belongs in the real world.

Software alone will not retool factories, grids, clinics, or supply chains. We back founders who treat physics as a feature, not a constraint.

The next decade of leverage will be won by people who design for matter, energy, and time—not just screens.

Fellowship partners early, before the demo day polish, when the hard problems still live in prototypes, process windows, and field trials. We write concentrated checks, stay close to the build cycle, and help founders assemble the operators, customers, and follow-on capital physical companies need.

We look for technical depth, moral seriousness, and a taste for problems that feel slightly too large. Beautifully unreasonable is not a slogan—it is a filter.

Atoms compound

When a system touches the physical world, improvements cascade into cost, reliability, and distribution in ways pure software rarely matches.

Founders own the stack

We prefer teams who can design, build, and debug end-to-end—firmware to fab, model to machine, protocol to plant floor.

Patience is strategy

Deep systems need multi-year arcs. We structure support so founders can pursue the hard path without theatrical milestones.

Partnership

How we work with founders

Capital is the start. The partnership is the product—practical help across product, hiring, customers, and the long financing path physical companies require.

  1. 01

    First principles diligence

    We dig into architecture, process risk, unit economics, and go-to-market realism. Expect hard questions and fast, clear feedback.

  2. 02

    Conviction capital

    When we partner, we lead or co-lead with enough capital to reach a meaningful technical and commercial proof point.

  3. 03

    Operator access

    We introduce domain operators, early design partners, and specialists across manufacturing, regulatory, and field deployment.

  4. 04

    Follow-on craft

    We help sequence Series A narratives around evidence the physical world respects: yield, uptime, safety, and paid pilots that convert.

What we look for

A crisp signal that this team can outbuild and outthink the problem over a decade, not a quarter.

  • Founders with hands-on technical ownership of the hard path
  • A wedge customers will pay for before the full vision lands
  • Clear physics, process, or distribution advantage
  • Ethics and safety treated as design inputs
  • Willingness to share the real roadmap, risks included
