The journal

Room for the long argument

Margin Quarterly was founded on a simple editorial wager: that cultural criticism still matters when it is given space—literal margins, patient sentences, and design that does not compete with the reading.

We publish four issues a year. Our writers come from criticism, reportage, design, and correspondence. We do not chase the news cycle; we ask what the culture is practicing when no one is watching the headline.

The name is deliberate. Margin is where annotations live, where books leave room for the reader, and where an idea can still change its mind.
