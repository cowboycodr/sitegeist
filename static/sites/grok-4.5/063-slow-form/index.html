How we work

Slow is not a style. It is a method.

We mill boards only after they have rested, cut joinery by hand where machines would rush the grain, and oil every surface until the wood accepts light evenly. Nothing leaves the bench until it can be lived with for a generation.

  1. Select Timber from managed local stands, matched for density and figure.
  2. Shape Templates refined over years; edges eased by hand so they invite touch.
  3. Join Mortise, tenon, and peg—structures that tighten with humidity and time.
  4. Number Each piece is stamped, recorded, and released with care notes for its owner.

Materials we trust

  • Ash Pale, resilient, open grain
  • Oak Hard wearing, warm ray fleck
  • Walnut Deep tone, soft polish
  • Cherry Blushes amber with light

Finishes are plant-based hardwax oils. No plastic laminates. Hardware is solid brass, replaceable.

The workshop

A small room full of tools and light

Slow Form is a two-person bench shop on a quiet industrial street. Boards season upstairs. Assembly happens downstairs beside a north window that keeps color honest. Visitors are welcome by appointment—bring questions about grain, finish, and fit.

  • Hours Tue–Sat, 10–17
  • Appointments Preferred for fittings
  • Lead time 6–14 weeks by piece
