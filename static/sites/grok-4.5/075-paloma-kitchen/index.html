The fire

Plates kissed by flame, finished with citrus.

Paloma is a neighborhood kitchen built around an open hearth. We pull seafood and vegetables through high heat, then brighten them with lime, bitter orange, and herbs from the courtyard garden.

Come for a long lunch under the vines, or stay late when the mezcal list opens up and the room glows copper. No dress code—just an appetite and a little time.
