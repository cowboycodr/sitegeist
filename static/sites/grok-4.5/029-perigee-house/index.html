Perigee House rides a modular spine with redundant life support, solar wings, and a rotating observation torus for partial gravity dining.

Habitation spine

Twelve suite modules dock along a pressurized corridor with dual emergency egress paths. Each cabin seals independently; guests never share air with public spaces unless they choose the lounge.

Observation torus

Docking node

Sample stay

Seventy-two hours above weather

A three-night orbit is our signature length—long enough for your vestibular system to settle, short enough to feel like pure wonder.

  1. 01

    Ascent & arrival

    Ferry from the equatorial spaceport. After hard-dock and a guided unseal, your concierge walks you through handhold etiquette, suit orientation, and your first Earth-rise from the arrival cupola.

  2. 02

    Station morning

    Breakfast in the torus as sixteen sunrises stack into one long day. Optional: guided free-float in the Zero Gallery, or a quiet hour in your suite with the limb of Earth as a living wallpaper.

  3. 03

    Deep night-side

    Private observation for aurora season (when the magnetosphere cooperates). Multi-course dinner at the Nadir Table, then sleep under circadian light set to your home city.

  4. 04

    Return burn

    Farewell briefing, photo archive of your orbit path, and a soft re-entry ferry that ends with champagne at sea-level pressure— Earth still looking different forever.
