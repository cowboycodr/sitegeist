From the open ground

Veldt Parfums distills the character of wide, wind-shaped places — sun-bleached stems, wet stone after a sudden storm, and the faint trace of fire carried over the ridge. Each composition is a map drawn in scent.

From the field

Field notes

12 September

Grass before the heat

Walked the northern ridge at first light. The stems still held last night’s coolness; by noon they would smell of toast and dust. We captured that hinge hour — green fading into gold — as the opening of Dry Grassland.

3 October

Basalt after rain

A brief storm left the dark rock steaming. The air tasted metallic and green at once. Mineral Rain is that interval: the moment before the sun returns and erases the coolness.

18 November

Smoke without fire

Someone burned brush two valleys over. By the time the wind reached us, only a soft resin remained. Distant Smoke keeps that restraint — ember, not blaze.

The practice

An atelier of open air

We work slowly, from place rather than from trend. Materials are chosen for their fidelity to landscape — true hay absolute over synthetic “grass,” iris root for the cool of wet stone, a measured thread of birch for smoke that has already thinned.

Each bottle is numbered by landscape, not by season. Wear them alone, or layer grassland over rain when the weather turns.
