What is Finale

A festival past the last frame

Interfaces used to be the destination. Now they are the foyer. Finale gathers artists, musicians, coders, performers, and theorists who treat the screen as material — not a window, but a stage, a score, a field of play.

Across three days, one hundred visions unfold in parallel: generative rooms, live-coded concerts, networked performances, and long-form conversations that refuse easy summaries. You arrive from anywhere. You leave with a stranger map of the near future.

  • 01 Signal Works that transmit feeling through systems, noise, and light.
  • 02 Syntax Code as craft, instrument, and public language.
  • 03 Afterimage What remains when the interface goes dark.

Access

Everywhere is the venue

How to attend

Finale is distributed by design. Enter from any device with a browser. High-bandwidth sessions offer full audiovisual streams; low-bandwidth modes deliver text, stills, and audio stems so you never lose the thread.

Accessibility

Live captions on all mainstage talks. Audio descriptions for selected visual works. Keyboard-first navigation across the festival shell. Session notes published within 24 hours.

Tickets & pace

A single festival pass unlocks every track. Attend live, or move at your own tempo through the archive during October 08–10. No FOMO required — only curiosity.
