The movement

Architecture you can see, finishing you can feel.

Our calibers are designed as visible architecture. Bridges are hand-bevelled, plates are circular-grained, and every screw slot is aligned. The goal is not spectacle—it is coherence under the crystal and under the loupe.

  • Manual wind, deliberate pace A daily ritual that keeps the wearer connected to the instrument.
  • Free-sprung balance Stable rate across positions, regulated on the timing machine and by ear.
  • Patient assembly One watchmaker carries a caliber from raw kit to final timing certificate.
Visit the atelier

Atelier

Materials chosen to last a lifetime of wear.

Steel that ages honestly, titanium that disappears on the wrist, dials printed and guillochéd in short runs, straps cut from vegetable-tanned leather. Nothing is rushed into production.

Case architecture

Proportioned for wrist presence without excess mass. Lugs are integrated so straps sit flush and the watch wears smaller than its diameter suggests.

Dial discipline

Indices, type, and hands are balanced for instant legibility. Color is restrained so the instrument reads in any light without competing with the wearer.

Human finishing

Bevels, anglage, and black polish are applied by hand. Machine tools establish geometry; the final character comes from the bench.

Provenance

A small house with a long horizon.

Meridian Works was founded to build watches that hold their place—on the wrist, in a collection, and across decades of service. We produce in limited annual volumes so every movement can be regulated, every case can be inspected, and every owner can speak with the people who made their watch.
