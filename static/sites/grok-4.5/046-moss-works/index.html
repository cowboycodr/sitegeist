Who we are

Landscapes that work with nature, not against it

Moss Works designs ecological gardens for homes, campuses, and civic spaces. We treat every site as a living system — soil, water, pollinators, people — and shape plantings that grow richer over time.

Our work favors regionally adapted plants, layered structure, and rooms for both wildlife and everyday life. The result is a landscape that feels wild at the edges and welcoming at the center.

  • Biodiversity first

    Every plan multiplies habitat — nectar, shelter, seed, and seasonal structure for birds, bees, and soil life.

  • Water-wise design

    Rain gardens, permeable paths, and deep-rooted plantings keep moisture on site and reduce runoff.

  • Room for people

    Meadow edges frame seating, paths invite wandering, and productive corners feed the kitchen as well as the canopy.

Planting philosophy

Design for succession, not just opening day

“A good garden gets more interesting every year — denser habitat, wiser structure, less work.”

  1. Listen to the site

    We map sun, soil, water paths, wind, and existing life before a single plant is chosen.

  2. Build layered plant communities

    Canopy, understory, shrub, groundcover, and root — each layer supports the next.

  3. Invite people in

    Paths, thresholds, and resting spots turn ecology into a daily experience, not a fenced exhibit.

  4. Steward, then step back

    Early care establishes the system; later years need less intervention as the garden finds its balance.
