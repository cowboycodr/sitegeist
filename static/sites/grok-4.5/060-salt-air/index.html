The inn

Weathered timber, open windows, unhurried days

Salt Air sits where the shore path meets a small harbor village. We keep twelve rooms, a kitchen that follows the catch, and a porch that catches every shift of wind off the water.

There is no resort program here—only linen that smells faintly of sun, coffee poured slowly, and keys that open onto the sound of the tide. Come for a night or a week; leave with sand still in your shoes.

Open windows

Every room faces air and light—cross-breeze by design, shutters when the weather turns.

Slow mornings

Breakfast until eleven. No rush to the next activity—just the tide chart and a newspaper.

Weathered timber

Floors and beams that have known decades of salt air. Soft underfoot, honest to the eye.

Beyond the porch

Explore the coast

Walk, paddle, or simply watch the light change. We keep tide tables at the desk and loan binoculars without ceremony.

01

Shore path

A packed-earth walk from the inn to the point—about forty minutes if you do not stop for shells.

02

Harbor village

Bookshop, chandlery, and a coffee counter that knows most of the fleet by name. Four minutes on foot.

03

Kayak cove

Calm water in the morning lee. Rentals two minutes down the lane; we hold your room key while you paddle.

04

Evening light

West-facing rocks below the inn. Bring a sweater. The last gulls go quiet just after the lamps come on.

At the inn

Small comforts, kept simple

What we offer is practical and close at hand—nothing you have to schedule weeks ahead.

  • Morning kitchen

    Coffee, fruit, warm bread, and eggs to order until eleven.

  • Wood-fired lounge

    A single fireplace, deep chairs, and board games with missing pieces that still work.

  • Porch & hammocks

    Blankets on pegs. Screens that keep the soft evening insects at bay.

  • Bicycles

    Six sturdy bikes for the village loop. Helmets optional, maps free.

  • Work nook

    Quiet desk space with strong Wi‑Fi if the world insists on finding you.

  • Pet-friendly rooms

    Two garden rooms welcome well-mannered dogs. Bowl and towel included.
