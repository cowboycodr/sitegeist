From the program notes

Filmmakers and curators on why these films do not behave.

  • “We programmed the work that made us lean forward in the dark — not the work that already knew how to sell itself.”
    Rae Okada Artistic director, Frame Zero
  • “Independent does not mean unfinished. It means the cut still belongs to the person who risked the first frame.”
    M. Vale Director, Static Between Stations
  • “If a film needs a franchise map to be understood, it is already someone else’s product. We want the opposite.”
    Samir Benali Guest programmer
  • “Sound first, plot second. If the room is listening, story will follow — or refuse to. Both are allowed.”
    Imani Cole Sound designer & juror

About the festival

Cinema that resets the counter

Frame Zero is a living festival program for independent films — features, shorts, hybrids, and restorations that refuse the expected. We gather sharp new voices, stubborn crafts, and audiences willing to sit with stories that do not come pre-explained.

Each season is a temporary city of screens: city cinemas, black-box rooms, and late-night halls where the house lights drop for work that still surprises the people who made it.

  • No formula Program by curiosity, not market category.
  • New voices First features and radical shorts share the center.
  • Living slate The program updates as prints, guests, and cities arrive.

Find a seat. Lose the formula.

Season 10 runs across six cities with day tickets, night passes, and open-caption screenings marked in the program.
