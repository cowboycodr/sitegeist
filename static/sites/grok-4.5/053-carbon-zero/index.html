Methodology

Transparent math, not a mystery box

Carbon Zero follows the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Scope 3 guidance. Every total is the product of activity data, an emission factor, and documented assumptions— all visible to your team and external reviewers.

Location-based and market-based Scope 2 are calculated side by side. Uncertainty bands flag spend-based estimates so primary data can replace them over time.

  • Global Warming Potentials published with each inventory period
  • Dual-unit tracking (activity + CO₂e) for every line item
  • Boundary definitions locked per reporting entity
