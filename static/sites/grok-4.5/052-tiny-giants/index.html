Our promise

Kids lead. Grown-ups follow the wonder.

Tiny Giants is a children’s museum built around curiosity, not corridors of “do not touch.” We design for small bodies, big feelings, and the glorious chaos of learning by doing.

  • ✋
    Hands first Every exhibit invites climbing, stacking, pouring, or performing—not watching from a rope line.
  • 💛
    Magnificent messes welcome Art, water, and dirt happen here on purpose. We clean; you create.
  • 🌈
    Everyone belongs Quiet hours, free sensory kits, and staff trained for inclusive play.

For the littlest giants

Soft play nooks, nursing-friendly lounges, and toddler-height everything mean babies and crawlers get a real museum day too.

For the grown-up sidekicks

Clear sightlines, sturdy seating, and exhibits that reward adult curiosity—because wonder is not age-limited.

School & group visits

Field trips include a facilitator-led opener, free-explore time, and a take-home “question kit.” Bookings open three months ahead.

Plan your visit

Come ready to get gloriously busy.

We’re a full-day playground for the mind. Arrive with socks for soft-play zones, clothes you don’t mind paint on, and an appetite for questions.

Quiet hour
First Saturday monthly, 8:30–10:00 — reduced sound and crowd for a calmer start
Do I need to book tickets ahead?

Weekdays are usually walk-up friendly. Weekends and school holidays fill up—reserve a timed entry online or at the door when available.

Can adults visit without children?

Yes during public hours if accompanying a child, or join our monthly Grown-Up Play Night (21+) for a mess-optional evening.

Is food allowed?

Picnic tables live in the courtyard. Water bottles welcome on the floor; sticky snacks stay in the café zone.
