Our materials

Nothing new from the earth that was not already given back.

We melt down reclaimed gold, silver, and bronze—old plate, industrial scrap, heirloom stock—then refine it in-house. Stones arrive with mine-to-studio documentation, chosen for character over polish.

  • Recycled metals Closed-loop refining · 100% reclaimed feedstock
  • Traceable stones Mine-level provenance · minimal cutting
  • Natural finishes Oxidation, hammer marks, left-as-cast edges

Process

Slow pressure. Honest marks.

  1. 01

    Source

    Metals reclaimed; stones selected for fracture, inclusion, and origin papers.

  2. 02

    Form

    Wax carving and open-face casting—surfaces left rough where the mold insists.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Hand filing, selective polish, controlled oxidation. Imperfection is kept on purpose.

  4. 04

    Release

    Small drops only. Each piece numbered, boxed in reclaimed wood fiber.

The atelier

A quiet bench between stone and fire.

Rare Earth is a two-person studio working in limited editions. We design for weight in the hand, for edges you can feel, and for jewelry that ages the way rock faces age—better, not cleaner.

Studio visits by appointment · Ships worldwide in recycled packaging
