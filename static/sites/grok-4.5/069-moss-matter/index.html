From waste to form

A cultivation process, not a factory line

We inoculate agricultural byproducts with selected mycelium, then let the fungus bind fibers into a solid body. Heat stops growth; the result is light, strong, and ready for life after use.

  1. 01

    Collect & mill

    Regional hemp hurd, wheat straw, and brewery spent grain are cleaned, dried, and sized for consistent structure.

  2. 02

    Inoculate

    Sterilized feedstock meets living culture in reusable molds. Growth rooms hold humidity and temperature in a narrow band.

  3. 03

    Grow the form

    Over one to two weeks, hyphae knit the mass into a continuous composite that holds the mold’s geometry.

  4. 04

    Dry & finish

    Gentle heat deactivates the organism. Surfaces may be sanded, plant-dyed, or sealed with food-safe biopolymers.

Third trial with a local brewery’s spent grain. Density improved 18% versus straw baseline without added binders. Color settled into a warm umber after low-heat drying—promising for interior panels that want a softer visual weight.

The studio

Grown for the products you already make

Moss & Matter partners with packaging engineers, fashion houses, and interior studios who want circular materials without waiting on a distant commodity market. We co-develop molds, finish recipes, and end-of-life guidance so every piece can return to soil with confidence.

  • Custom molds Geometry from your CAD or physical prototype, grown at pilot and production scale.
  • Material passports Feedstock origin, strain family, and compost guidance packaged with every batch.
  • Closed-loop takeback Optional recovery of end-of-life parts for industrial composting partners.

Principles we grow by

  1. Use waste streams first
  2. Keep chemistry simple and named
  3. Design for soil, not landfill
  4. Let batches keep their character
