The atelier

Sculpted form. Liquid surface.

Velvet Orbit is a directional label built on a single contradiction: garments that feel carved from mass, yet move like fluid metal under studio light.

Every Aphelion piece is cut from proprietary metallized textiles — foil jersey, chrome satin, alloy mesh — then finished with hand-set structural seams that hold a silhouette against gravity.

  • Mass without bulk Internal architecture replaces padding.
  • Light as material Surfaces are graded to catch, bend, and release reflection.
  • Orbit as cut Seams follow elliptical paths around the body.
