The platform

One stack for every public service journey

Civic Stack gives local teams modular building blocks—so permitting, benefits, hearings, and neighborhood services feel coherent to residents and manageable for staff.

Plain-language services

Guided applications, readable status updates, and multilingual content patterns that reduce call-center load.

Workflows that match reality

Configure approvals, inspections, and handoffs to mirror how your departments already work—then improve them.

Transparent by design

Audit trails, public dashboards, and open data exports keep elected leaders and residents aligned on progress.

Security for public trust

Role-based access, encryption at rest, and privacy defaults tuned for sensitive resident information.

Public principles

Infrastructure that belongs to the public

Civic Stack is engineered for longevity: open interfaces, plain documentation, and procurement-friendly packaging for small teams.

  1. Accessible first Keyboard, screen reader, and low-bandwidth experiences are requirements—not polish.
  2. Open by default APIs, schemas, and design tokens ship with every module so integrators stay free.
  3. Human-scale ops Role training, change logs, and sandboxes help lean IT teams ship safely.
  4. Measurable outcomes Service-level dashboards report wait times, completion rates, and equity gaps.
