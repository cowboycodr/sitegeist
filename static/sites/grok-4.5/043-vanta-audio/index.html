Series One · Open reference

Philosophy

Sound lives in the silence around it.

Most headphones fill every frequency. Series One leaves room. We tune for the breath before a note, the decay of a string, the width of a room you can almost walk into.

“The space between notes is where intention becomes emotion. That is the register we design for.” Vanta Acoustic Lab

Craft

Tuned for how music feels.

Three pillars guide every Series One decision—from diaphragm tension to the materials that touch your skin for hours.

Depth

Layered imaging that places instruments in a stable, three-dimensional field— not louder, just truer to the stage they were recorded on.

Texture

Micro-detail without glare. Breath, bow noise, and room tone stay present so recordings feel tactile rather than polished hollow.

Weight

Low-end that supports, not dominates. Series One carries emotional mass in the midbass and lower mids—where songs land in the chest.

Engineering

Built like a studio instrument.

An open acoustic architecture, low-mass planar transducers, and a suspension that disappears on the head. Every element is chosen to widen the soundstage without thinning the body of the music.

  • Driver 48 mm planar magnetic
  • Response 12 Hz – 40 kHz
  • Impedance 18 Ω nominal
  • Sensitivity 96 dB / mW
  • Weight 328 g
  • Cable Detachable dual-entry OFC
01 · Acoustics

Open reference field

A ventilated rear chamber and tuned damping keep reflections under control, so the stage feels airy without the thinness open-backs often trade for.

02 · Transducer

Low-mass planar array

A dual-magnet planar driver with a precision-etched diaphragm recovers transients quickly and holds bass pitch—clarity without fatigue.

03 · Fit

Silent suspension

Memory-foam pads and a self-adjusting headband distribute weight evenly. Long sessions stay comfortable so you forget the hardware.
