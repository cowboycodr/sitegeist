Process

From survey to first kilowatt-hour.

A deliberate sequence keeps design quality intact from proposal through commissioning.

  1. 01

    Site reading

    Solar path analysis, structural review, and material context—photographs, drawings, and on-site walk-throughs.

  2. 02

    Placement study

    Array geometry tuned for yield and appearance. We present two to three compositions with energy models.

  3. 03

    Fabrication

    Frames and mounts cut to order. Modules batch-matched for color. Hardware kits labeled by zone.

  4. 04

    Install & tune

    Certified crews, clean cable work, and a commissioning report you can keep offline forever.
