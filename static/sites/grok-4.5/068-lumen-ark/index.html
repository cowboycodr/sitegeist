Method

Approach

We begin with orientation, shadow, and the length of the Danish day. Form follows the path of light before it follows programme.

  1. 01

    Daylight first

    Massing, openings, and depth are calibrated to seasonal sun angles so rooms stay luminous without harsh contrast.

  2. 02

    Quiet material

    Brick, timber, lime, and stone—few finishes, honest joints, surfaces that age with weather rather than against it.

  3. 03

    Measured threshold

    Entries, courts, and galleries slow the shift from street to interior so light and sound settle before use begins.

Practice

Studio

Lumen Ark is a Copenhagen practice of architects and makers working across culture buildings, housing, and careful reuse. We keep a small atelier so every project holds a continuous hand from sketch to site.

Our rooms favour northern and eastern light, deep reveals, and materials that invite touch. We collaborate with structural engineers, landscape designers, and craftspeople who share a patience for detail.

  • 12 people in the atelier
  • 28 built projects
  • 4 countries of work

Writing

Studio notes

Short reflections from the drawing board and the building site.

November 2024

On winter light in brick rooms

In November the sun never climbs high enough to fill a courtyard. We deepen reveals and polish floor planes so what little light arrives is held and shared.

June 2024

Thresholds that slow the city

A gallery door need not announce itself. A half-level change, a change of acoustic, a strip of sky—small moves that mark arrival without noise.

February 2024

Measuring the day before the plan

We model solar paths before room layouts. Programme finds its place inside the light, not the other way around.
