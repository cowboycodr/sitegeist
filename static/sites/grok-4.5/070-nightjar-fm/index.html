About the station

The signal after midnight

Nightjar FM is an independent night-only broadcaster. We favor music that needs room: ambient, experimental, and left-field work that does not rush the listener.

The nightjar is a nocturnal bird — unassuming by day, unmistakable after dark. Our frequency follows the same pattern: quiet during daylight hours, fully open once the city thins out.

  • Human-hosted blocks with long mixes and few interruptions
  • No commercial clusters, no drive-time templates
  • Listener-supported, desk-built, and deliberately small
