How it works

One album. One room. Real talk between tracks.

  1. 01

    Pick or host

    Join an open room or start a session with an album you love. Invite friends with a short room code.

  2. 02

    Listen in sync

    Everyone hears the same side, same second. No skipping chaos—just the album as the artist sequenced it.

  3. 03

    Talk in the gaps

    Chat opens between tracks and during side breaks. Trade liner notes, stories, and what to queue next.

Built for the hang

Everything a listening party needs—nothing it doesn’t.

Album-first playback

Rooms are bound to a full album. Discoveries stay intentional instead of dissolving into endless shuffle.

Between-track chat

Conversation pauses when the music starts, then reopens at natural breaks so nobody talks over the good part.

Friend circles

Private rooms for your crew, public rooms for curious ears. Presence is visible without becoming a performance.

Liner-note boards

Pin producer credits, first-listen memories, and “play this next” recommendations right on the room wall.

Session recaps

After the final track, save a recap of who joined, favorite moments, and the albums people queued from chat.

Crossfade invites

Share a room code that lands guests at the current track—no awkward “wait, where are you?” scrambling.
