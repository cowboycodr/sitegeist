Why dual packs

Traction energy never steals from your lights or laptop. When the road pack is low, the house pack still runs climate, refrigeration, and the desk for days—then solar tops it while you work.

  • Isolated BMS per pack
  • Vehicle-to-cabin boost when needed
  • Shore charge overnight at 11 kW
