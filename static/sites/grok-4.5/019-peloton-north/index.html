The club

A collective, not a clubhouse

Peloton North started as a Thursday group that refused the shortest commute. We still meet on the same corner, still leave on time, and still take the longer road when the weather argues otherwise.

Membership means a place in the group chat, first call on limited kit drops, and a shared standard: ride clean, ride together, leave no one guessing.

  • All-weather, year-round calendar
  • Route notes shared 48 hours before roll-out
  • No-drop policy until the designated regroup
  • Skill nights for winter descending and group form

How it works

  1. Show up once Join any open ride. Introduce yourself at the meet point.
  2. Stay for three After three rides, request membership — free, invitation-backed.
  3. Own the cadence Propose routes, lead a Sunday, or help with kit fittings.

Capacity is capped so group size stays rideable. Waiting list opens each season.
