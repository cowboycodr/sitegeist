The archive

Stewarded, not owned

Folkways exists to keep traditional music audible in context: with the names of performers, the languages of origin, and the geographies that shaped each phrase.

Recordings enter the archive through community partnership, family gifts, and restored field collections. Playback here is for listening and study; rights remain with the source communities and depositors.
