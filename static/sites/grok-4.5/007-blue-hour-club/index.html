Atmosphere

The room

Blue Hour sits below street level in Wicker Park — brick, low ceilings, and a stage that faces a horseshoe of tables. The light stays amber. The bar pours until the last set ends.

We book searching jazz: modern, soul, and the improvisations that happen when residents and guests share the stand. No cover for the late set on weeknights. Reservations hold a table; walk-ins fill the rail.

  • Capacity 84 seated · standing rail at the bar
  • Sound Natural room · light reinforcement on vocals
  • Policy 21+ after 9 · quiet phones during sets

House notes

  1. Doors open at 9:00. First notes around 10:30.
  2. Tables are for two hours per reservation on busy nights.
  3. Kitchen closes at midnight; bar stays open through the late set.
  4. Tip the band if the music moved you — the jar is at the stage.
