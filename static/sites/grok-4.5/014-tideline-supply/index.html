Approach

Our approach

“If it only looks coastal, it does not ship. We cut for spray, salt, and the walk back.”

Tideline Supply designs durable coastal goods for people who measure a day by tide tables and trailheads, not store windows. Every pattern revision comes from remote breaks, ferry decks, and long exposure in mixed wet and dry weather.

We favor repairable hardware, quiet graphics, and materials that still work after a rinse in a parking lot. Water without walls means the map fades—and the kit has to keep up.

Field first Prototype on actual coastlines before colorways are locked.
Repairable Marine-grade hardware you can service, not throw away.
Quiet gear No shouty logos. The coast already has enough noise.
Seasoned cloth Fabrics chosen for salt, grit, and repeated wet cycles.
