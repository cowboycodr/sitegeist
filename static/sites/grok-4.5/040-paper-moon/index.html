Ongoing series

Three ways into the dark

Our house strands: overlooked classics, midnight oddities, and unforgettable new voices. Each series runs on a lunar calendar — new titles with every moon.

Overlooked classics

Studio orphans, regional masterpieces, and films that arrived too early or too late. Restored when we can; projected carefully always.

  • Print notes in the lobby
  • Archival intros on Fridays
  • 35mm preferred

Midnight oddities

Cult curios, genre mutations, and films that only make sense after midnight. Expect laughter, dread, and the occasional grocery aisle.

  • Doors at 21:45
  • Late-night menu
  • No spoilers in the lobby

New voices

First features, local premieres, and filmmakers still sharpening their light. We hold the room quiet so new work can land.

  • Q&As when possible
  • Sliding-scale Sundays
  • Filmmaker lounge access

Plan your visit

Harbor & 9th

A single-screen house with a deep blue curtain, paper tickets, and a lobby that smells faintly of orange oil and old film cement.

Paper Moon Cinema
914 Harbor Avenue
Open doors 45 minutes before showtime

Access

Step-free entry on Harbor, hearing loop at row G, companion seats on request. Large-print programs available at the box office.

House rules

Phones dark, conversations soft, late seating only until the first cut. Food from our bar is welcome; outside meals stay in the lobby.

Membership

Twelve months of member pricing, free midweek matinees, and first look at special prints. Inquire at the box office after any show.
