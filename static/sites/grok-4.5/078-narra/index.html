The method

From scattered capture to a narrative you can defend.

Most tools store knowledge. Narra arranges it into sequence, tension, and proof— the shape of a story your collaborators can follow.

  1. 01

    Gather without losing the trail

    Drop sources and clips into a library that preserves origin, quotes, and confidence—not just file names.

  2. 02

    Note in the margins of meaning

    Write observations next to evidence. Tags, questions, and open loops stay visible instead of buried in folders.

  3. 03

    Map the narrative spine

    Arrange beats into a map: setup, conflict, evidence, turn, and resolution. Drag claims until the argument holds.

  4. 04

    Share a living draft

    Invite collaborators onto the same map. Comments attach to beats, not endless document threads.

Overview

How a session feels.

A quiet, focused pass from capture to narrative spine—designed for deep work and clear collaboration, not notification noise.

  1. 0:00

    Import the stack

    Sources land with automatic excerpt anchors and readable titles.

  2. 0:04

    Sketch the spine

    Place five beats. Attach notes. Leave gaps where proof is thin.

  3. 0:11

    Pressure-test the turn

    Invite a collaborator. They challenge one claim; the map updates live.

  4. 0:18

    Export the story

    Outline, citation pack, and map snapshot leave together—ready for writing.

Capabilities

Everything that keeps a narrative honest.

Provenance lattice

Every claim retains a path back to page, timestamp, or speaker. Nothing floats free.

Collaborative beats

Co-edit the spine. Comments, assignments, and alternate frames sit on the map itself.

Story outlines

Generate a linear outline from the map when you are ready to write—or present—without retyping.

Confidence marks

Flag solid, contested, and speculative statements so readers know where the argument is soft.

Focus modes

Hide noise. Work only on open questions, only on unsupported claims, or only on one collaborator’s lane.

Local-first calm

Your library stays on device by default. Sync is intentional, not a constant background storm.
