Current show

Residual Light

Six commissioned works explore afterimage as method: phosphor trails, delayed color, and interfaces that remember your touch. Scroll slowly— each piece rewards patience and proximity.

Visitor notes

  • Format Browser-native experiences; no plugins.
  • Interaction Touch, mouse, and keyboard paths for every work.
  • Duration Allow 20–40 minutes for a full walkthrough.
  • Access Reduced-motion variants honor system preferences.

Collection

Luminous commissions

Each piece is built for the screen as site—not a scan of a canvas. Explore the current cycle of interactive light works.

Room 02 · Mira Chen

Phosphor Ledger

Gesture strokes write to a fading ledger that never fully erases—memory as material.

Room 03 · Leo Okonkwo

Slow Orbit Index

Celestial bodies drift on delayed clocks; selecting one reveals its afterimage twin.

Room 04 · Aya Noor

Frame Error Garden

A living grid that misregisters on purpose—pixels bloom where alignment fails.

Room 05 · Jun Park

Half-Life Choir

Soundless pulses visualize decay curves; hold a key to extend a note’s afterglow.

Room 06 · Sable Rivera

Veil of Seconds

Layered time surfaces slide at different rates—your presence slows the top veil.

Room 07 · Collective Null

Signal Remains

Broadcast noise rendered as quiet topography—find the quietest peak and stay.

Practitioners

Meet the artists

Afterimage partners with makers who treat code, light, and interaction as primary media—not as afterthoughts to physical work.

  • Mira Chen

    Generative systems · Taipei / Berlin

    Builds instruments that remember touch and forget on a schedule of her own design.

  • Leo Okonkwo

    Spatial interfaces · Lagos / London

    Maps orbital delay into navigable score—clocks you can walk through.

  • Aya Noor

    Pixel gardens · Cairo

    Cultivates misalignment as a formal language; gardens grow from error states.

  • Jun Park

    Sonic visualization · Seoul

    Translates decay envelopes into luminous choirs without emitting a single tone.

  • Sable Rivera

    Temporal media · Mexico City

    Stacks translucent time layers that respond to how long you remain present.

  • Collective Null

    Network art · Distributed

    An anonymous trio rendering quiet peaks inside broadcast residue.
