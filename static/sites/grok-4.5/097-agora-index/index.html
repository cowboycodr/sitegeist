    How it works

    From ledgers to signals

    Method in brief

    Transparent steps, no black boxes—designed for residents first.

    1. 1
      Collect public records

      Budgets, permits, GTFS performance, hearing minutes, and FOIA logs.

    2. 2
      Normalize to shared scales

      Each indicator is scored 0–100 with documented ceilings and floors.

    3. 3
      Weight by resident impact

      Housing and transit carry more weight where cost burden is highest.

    4. 4
      Publish hourly pulse

      Composite and pillar scores refresh from local sample packs offline.
