    The house

    Composed for the dark hours

    Nocturne is a small perfumery working only after sundown. Our raw materials are chosen for how they behave in low light: cool minerals that hold the day's warmth, resins that read like distant smoke, and flowers that open only once the garden has gone quiet.

    Nothing here is bright. Every composition is built to linger close to the skin, the way weather lingers on the far edge of a field before it arrives.
