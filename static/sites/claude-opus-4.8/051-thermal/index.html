The problem

Two thirds of the world's energy escapes as heat.

Furnaces, kilns, compressors and engines throw off enormous streams of thermal energy. Most of it vents to the sky. We built Thermal to catch it.

The technology

Capture, convert, return.

A closed thermal loop bolts onto existing exhaust. Nothing changes upstream; everything downstream gets cleaner.

  1. 01

    Capture

    High-efficiency exchangers draw heat from flue gas, cooling jackets and process streams without disturbing the line.

  2. 02

    Convert

    Organic Rankine turbines and thermoelectric stacks turn the temperature gap into grid-quality electricity.

  3. 03

    Return

    Clean power feeds back into the plant or the grid, while residual warmth is looped into district heat.
