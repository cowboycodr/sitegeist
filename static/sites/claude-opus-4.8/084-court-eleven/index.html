The four rituals

One membership, four ways to spend a morning.

Court Eleven is built around a simple loop — warm up, play, recover, linger. Everything you need for it lives on site.

01

Open play

Book by the hour or drop into a doubles rotation. Balanced matchmaking pairs you by level, not by luck.

Book by the hour
02

Coaching

Seven resident pros running privates, small-group clinics, and video breakdowns from beginner to tournament.

Privates & clinics
03

Recovery

Cold plunge, infrared sauna, and a stretch studio. Reset between sets or make it the whole visit.

Plunge & sauna
04

Hospitality

All-day café, natural wine, and a courtside kitchen. The clubhouse stays open long after the last point.
