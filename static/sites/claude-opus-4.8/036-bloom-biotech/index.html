The science

Three kingdoms of life, taught to heal.

Bloom's discovery engine treats living systems as collaborators. We borrow the mechanisms that cells, fungi, and plants already use to repair, defend, and regenerate — then direct them toward human disease.

Cellular therapy

Reprogrammed cells that sense their environment and secrete precisely the signals a damaged tissue needs to rebuild itself.

Fungal networks

Mycelial chemistry produces molecules synthetic labs struggle to make — antifungals, immunomodulators, and scaffolds that biodegrade on cue.

Plant intelligence

Plants defend themselves without a nervous system. We decode that distributed intelligence into drugs that adapt to the body's own signals.

The platform

From spore to clinic, on one living pipeline.

Every Bloom program runs the same loop — an evolutionary system that lets biology iterate faster than any purely computational design ever could.

Cultivate

Strains and cell lines are grown in adaptive bioreactors that record their own metabolic history.

Sequence

Multi-omics readouts map how each organism responds, building a searchable atlas of living behavior.

Direct

Closed-loop guidance nudges biology toward the therapeutic phenotype, generation after generation.

Translate

Candidates graduate into GMP manufacturing and human trials without leaving the platform.
