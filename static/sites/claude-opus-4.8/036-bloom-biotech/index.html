The living platform

Three kingdoms of biology, engineered into one therapeutic toolkit.

Bloom doesn't synthesize molecules and hope they stick. We program living systems to make, deliver, and adapt medicine inside the body — sourcing our building blocks from three domains of life.

Cellular

Engineered cells

Autologous cell lines reprogrammed to secrete regenerative signals on demand — dosing that listens to the tissue around it.

Mycelial

Fungal networks

Mycelium scaffolds grow custom matrices for tissue repair, threading nutrients and drug payloads through their branching architecture.

Botanical

Plant intelligence

Plants sense and route resources without a brain. We borrow those decentralized circuits to design therapies that self-regulate over time.

Why grown, not made

Living medicine adapts. Static molecules can't.

  • Responsive dosing. Cells read their microenvironment and release only what the tissue asks for.
  • Self-assembling scaffolds. Fungal matrices build structure in place — no synthetic polymers left behind.
  • Decentralized control. Plant-inspired logic keeps therapies stable without a central failure point.
  • Grown at scale. Bioreactors cultivate therapeutic tissue the way a greenhouse raises seedlings.
