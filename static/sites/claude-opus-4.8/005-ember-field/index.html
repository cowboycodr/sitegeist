    Meet the producers

    We visit each farm before the harvest and pay above the season's reference price. Three of the people behind this season's table.

    1,950 m

    Marisol Tenório

    Nariño, Colombia

    Shade-grown Caturra on a steep ridge above the Juanambú. Marisol ferments in sealed tanks overnight, coaxing out the stone-fruit sweetness we chase every year.

    2,100 m

    Guji, Ethiopia

    Heirloom varieties dried on raised beds and turned by hand every hour of daylight. His washing station is a cooperative of forty smallholders.

    The ritual

    A pour-over, unhurried

    The way we brew on the counter each morning. Numbers are a starting point — trust your palate over the scale.

    1. 01

      Weigh & rinse

      22 g of coffee, ground medium-coarse. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and tip it out to warm the vessel.

    2. 02

      Bloom

      Pour 50 g of 94 °C water. Wait forty-five seconds while the grounds swell and breathe out their gas.

    3. 03

      Pour in stages

      Add water in slow spirals to 360 g total, keeping the bed level. The whole draw should finish near three minutes.

    4. 04

      Rest & taste

      Let it cool a minute. Sweetness arrives as the cup drops below scalding — that is when the origin shows itself.
