About the station

Nightjar FM is a listener-run, non-commercial signal that only wakes when the city sleeps. We favour music that rewards patience: the ambient, the experimental, and the difficult-to-shelve.

There are no adverts, no algorithm, and no daytime programming. Just a handful of hosts, a modest transmitter, and the long quiet stretch between midnight and dawn.
