The Bloomline idea

We arrange flowers the way a sculptor arranges space.

Every composition starts with a line — the gesture a stem wants to make — and builds outward until the negative space is doing as much work as the blooms.

i.

Seasonal, always

We buy what the growers cut this week. If it isn't in season within reach of London, it isn't in the vase.

ii.

Line before mass

Structure comes first. Colour and volume answer to the silhouette, never the other way around.

iii.

Made to be lived with

Sculptural doesn't mean fragile. Our arrangements are built to hold their shape on a real table, all week.
