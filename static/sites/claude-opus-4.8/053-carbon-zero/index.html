Every scope, one ledger

Carbon Zero reconciles direct combustion, purchased energy, and the sprawling value chain into a single audited number you can defend.

Scope 1

Direct emissions

On-site fuel, company vehicles, and fugitive gases metered at the source and reconciled against invoices.

Scope 2

Purchased energy

Electricity, steam, and cooling tracked with location- and market-based factors, credited for verified renewables.

Scope 3

Value chain

Spend-based and supplier-specific data across fifteen categories, prioritised by materiality and confidence.

Methodology

Numbers you can stand behind in an audit, a board meeting, or a disclosure.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Activity data flows in from meters, ERP spend, and fleet telematics into a single normalized model.

  2. 02

    Convert

    Emission factors from recognized datasets translate every activity into tonnes of CO₂ equivalent, with uncertainty tracked.

  3. 03

    Reduce

    Marginal abatement curves rank interventions so the cheapest tonnes fall first and targets stay credible.
