Open science

Built to be shared, not owned

Raw waveforms, detection labels, and station metadata are public by default. Reproduce a finding, train a classifier, or wire the stream into your own study.

  • Open data — every archive downloadable under CC BY 4.0.
  • Open hardware — buildable hydrophone reference design.
  • Open review — detections annotated by a global community.
