Why a stillroom?

Once, a stillroom was a quiet space in a house set aside for slow, careful work — steeping, distilling, letting things become themselves. We kept the name for what it points to: a small room in the day that belongs to no one's urgency.

Nothing here streams, tracks, or hurries. The light breathes, the count is patient, and when you close the tab the room simply waits.

“Almost everything will work again if you unplug it for a few minutes, including you.”
