Why the south pole

A habitat that runs itself before we arrive.

Selene M01 lands an uncrewed habitat in permanent sunlight near the lunar south pole, then commissions its own power, thermal, and life-support loops. When the first crew follows, the address is already home.

01

Sunlit power

Peaks of near-eternal light keep the array charged through the long lunar night, banking energy in regenerative fuel cells.

02

Water in the dark

Shadowed craters hold ice. Our rover prospects, extracts, and electrolyses it into breathable air and propellant.

03

Regolith shelter

An autonomous printer heaps local dust over the pressure shell, shielding crew from radiation and micrometeorites.
