Our approach

We begin with the site, not the plan.

01

Read the ground

Contours, light, water, and habit. Every project starts with weeks of quiet fieldwork and a notebook full of measurements.

02

Build with restraint

Timber, rammed earth, lime, and local stone. We design for a long, repairable life and a light touch on the land.

03

Leave room for weather

Thresholds, shade, and shelter that let people live with the seasons rather than seal themselves against them.

The practice

A small studio, working slowly.

Field Notes is a landscape-led architecture practice of nine people. We take on a handful of projects each year so that every one has time to find its place. We draw by hand before we draw by machine, and we return to each building years after it is done to learn how it has weathered.
