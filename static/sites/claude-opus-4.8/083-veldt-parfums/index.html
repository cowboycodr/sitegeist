From the field notes

How a landscape becomes a scent

  1. i

    Walk it

    We spend days in a place at every hour, recording what the air does when the light changes and the ground cools.

  2. ii

    Capture it

    Headspace readings are taken over living grass, wet stone, and drifting smoke, then matched to natural absolutes and molecules.

  3. iii

    Compose it

    The perfumer builds an accord that opens, breathes, and settles the way the landscape itself moves through a day.
