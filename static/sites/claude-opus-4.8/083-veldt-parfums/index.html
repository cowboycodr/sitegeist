Field Notes

Written where the grass meets the sky

Our perfumer walks the same reserve through the turning year, recording the air. These are the entries that became bottles.

See how they are built
  1. May

    Noon, high veldt. Heat lifts a dusty sweetness from the seed heads — like tea left in the sun. This is where Dry Season begins.

  2. Nov

    First rain. The whole plain exhales at once. Cold minerals, green stems, and something almost metallic underfoot.

  3. Aug

    Dusk, ridge line. Smoke on the horizon, soft and resinous, never sharp. It reads as warmth, not alarm.

The Craft

Structured like weather

Every Veldt fragrance is built as a three-part arc so the landscape moves across the day on your skin.

Top — the horizon

Bright, airy openings that arrive first and fade like morning light: ozone, straw, green citrus.

Heart — the plain

The body of the scent where florals, clay, and hay settle in and hold for hours.

Base — the earth

Woods, resins, and mineral vetiver that ground the composition long after dark.

Begin at the horizon

Discovery sets carry all three landscapes in 8 ml vials — a full day of the veldt to sample at home.
