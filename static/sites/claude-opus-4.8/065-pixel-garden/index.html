Worlds we grew

Three small games, each with one strange rule at its heart.

  • Cozy puzzler

    Lantern Moth

    Guide a paper moth home by rearranging the light. Everything you touch keeps glowing until dawn.

    Rule: light never turns off.

  • Exploration

    Tide Cartographer

    Map an island that rewrites its own coastline every time the tide turns. Your notes are the only thing that stays.

    Rule: the map is never finished.

  • Sandbox

    Root & Rumor

    Whisper to plants and they whisper back — and to each other. Rumors travel root to root across the whole valley.

    Rule: secrets spread while you sleep.

A four-person greenhouse

We are two designers, an artist, and a composer working out of a converted greenhouse. We make games slowly, on purpose, and we finish every one we plant.

Our worlds are hand-tended: pixel by pixel, note by note. We build for the moment a player leans in and says “wait, what happens if…”
