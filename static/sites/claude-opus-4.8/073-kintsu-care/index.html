How it works

Three quiet steps, repeated at your pace

Kintsu is named for kintsugi — the art of mending what's broken with gold. Small daily acts of care become the seams that hold a harder week together.

  1. Check in

    Take two minutes to notice how you actually are. A short, judgment-free prompt turns a vague feeling into something you can work with.

  2. Reflect & practice

    Kintsu offers a gentle reflection and a matching tool — a breathing pace, a grounding list, or a note to bring to therapy.

  3. Carry it forward

    Patterns you notice travel with you into sessions, so your time with a therapist starts from what's real, not from scratch.
