The thesis

We fund the founders who take the world literally.

Software ate the world. Now the world is asking for the hardware back. We invest at the seam between atoms and code — where progress is slow, capital is shy, and the winners define a decade.

  1. 01

    Conviction over consensus

    We write the first check the room is afraid to. If the idea is obvious to everyone, it is probably too late to be interesting.

  2. 02

    Engineers, not decks

    We read the physics, the bill of materials, and the failure modes. Diligence is a conversation between builders, not a scoring rubric.

  3. 03

    Patient by design

    Real things take real time. Our fund is structured for the ten-year arc of a hard problem, not the eighteen-month markup.

  4. 04

    Useful when it counts

    Recruiting the impossible hire, sourcing the rare part, standing in the fab at 2am. We are operators who happen to hold equity.
