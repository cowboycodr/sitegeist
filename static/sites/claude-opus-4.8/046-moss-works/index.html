Planting philosophy

Design that defers to the land

We treat a site as a living system first and a canvas second. Four principles guide every plan we draw.

01

Read before you draw

Soil pits, light studies and a species survey come before a single line on the plan.

02

Native-led, not native-only

A backbone of local species with resilient companions that will hold up as the climate shifts.

03

Water where it falls

Rain gardens, swales and deep-rooted planting keep every drop on site instead of in a drain.

04

Room for people

Paths, thresholds and clearings so the wild feels invited, not off-limits.

How we work

From bare ground to biodiversity

A clear four-season path from first walk to a garden that maintains itself.

Survey

We map soil, water, light and the plants and creatures already at home on your site.

Design

A planting plan and habitat strategy, drawn to grow richer — not tidier — over time.

Plant

Our crews establish structure, groundcover and seed in the right season for each layer.

Steward

Two years of light-touch guidance until the landscape holds its own ground.
