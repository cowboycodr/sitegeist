Materials

Only what earns its weight

Recycled 18k gold
Refined from reclaimed metal, alloyed in the studio to a warm, unbleached tone.
Sterling silver
Cast solid and left to oxidize gently, so each piece deepens with wear.
Found stone
River pebbles and rough shards, set unpolished, chosen one by one.
Lost wax
Every object begins as a carved wax, burned away once — never reused, never molded twice.

The atelier

Made on a single bench in Oltrarno

Across the Arno, in a room that smells of beeswax and flux, each Oro Still object is carved, invested, poured, and finished by the same pair of hands. We cast in the morning light and let the metal rest before it is filed.

  1. 01 Carve the wax freehand, one original per piece.
  2. 02 Invest, burn out, and pour molten metal by hand.
  3. 03 Rest, file, and finish only where the hand insists.

Keeping

Carried, not stored

These are working objects. Wear softens them; a warm cloth and a quiet drawer are all the care they ask. Each order arrives with a hand-numbered card and a lifetime of repair from the bench that made it.

“A piece should outlast the reason you bought it, and become the reason you keep it.” — Oro Still
