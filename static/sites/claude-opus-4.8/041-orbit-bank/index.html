Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is my money safe with Orbit?

Orbit is a demonstration website. In a real deployment, eligible deposits would be protected up to the relevant scheme limit and funds held with regulated partner banks.

What does it cost?

The core account is free. Premium plans add travel perks and higher fee-free limits for a flat monthly price — with no surprise charges.

Which currencies are supported?

Hold, receive, and exchange across 40+ currencies, with local account details available in the most-used ones.

How long does opening take?

Most people finish in under five minutes from a phone, with identity verification handled in-app.
