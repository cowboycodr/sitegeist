The station

A rotating ring, engineered for stillness.

Three pressurized decks share a single curved promenade. Structure handled by aerospace tolerances; comfort handled by people who have run the world's best hotels on the ground.

The Promenade

A 60-metre viewing gallery of curved fused-silica glass. Walk the full arc of the horizon while the terminator line sweeps beneath your feet.

Gentle spin

The habitation ring turns at a whisper to hold a comfortable one-third gravity — enough to pour a coffee, light enough to feel weightless.

Closed loop

Water, air, and warmth recirculate through the same quiet systems that keep a research station alive — refined for guests who never see them.

The approach

From launch pad to lobby in four steps.

  1. 01

    Ascent

    A crewed vehicle carries you to orbit in under nine minutes, with a flight director who has done it a hundred times before.

  2. 02

    Rendezvous

    You match the ring's orbit and glide the final metres to a soft, automated dock — no jolt, just a settle.

  3. 03

    Arrival

    Step through the airlock into a warm, lamplit lobby and hand your gravity legs off to the crew who caught them for you.

  4. 04

    Residency

    Settle into your suite as the promenade lights dim and the planet rolls a fresh sunrise beneath the glass.
