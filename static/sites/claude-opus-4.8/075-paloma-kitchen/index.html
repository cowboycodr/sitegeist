The Fire

Everything passes over flame.

Our kitchen is built around a single wood hearth. Mesquite and oak, lit at four, coaxed down to embers by six. Coastal fish arrives in the afternoon; by night it's kissed with citrus and smoke.

It's the kind of room where dinner turns into midnight — low light, a little music, mezcal poured slow.

  • Live-flame hearth, no gas line
  • Whole fish from Baja, daily
  • Masa nixtamalized in-house
