From clay to shelf

Slow work, done in daylight.

01

Thrown

Every form begins on the wheel with clay wedged by hand that same morning.

02

Glazed

Glazes are mixed from mineral oxides and tested in small tiles before a batch.

03

Fired

Two firings over three days settle colour and make each piece watertight for life.
