From the lab notebook

Obsessive pastry notes

We keep a running notebook on the bench. Tap a note to read what we changed and why.

No. 041 Why we cold-proof the croissants

Warm proofing raced the butter to melting before the dough could set. A slow overnight rest at 6 °C let the yeast work while the layers stayed distinct — the crumb went from bready to honeycomb.

No. 052 Hydration versus the season

Flour drinks differently in July. We nudged the sourdough up to 82% and shortened the bulk by twenty minutes so the humid kitchen didn't over-ferment it. Same loaf, adjusted for the weather.

No. 058 The beeswax on the canelé

Butter alone browned unevenly and stuck. A thin beeswax-and-butter mix on the copper molds gives that glassy, near-burnt shell without tearing the custard when we turn them out.

No. 063 Grinding cardamom to order

Pre-ground pods lose their top notes within a day. We crack them the morning of, so the buns smell like citrus and pine instead of dusty spice. Worth the extra ten minutes.
