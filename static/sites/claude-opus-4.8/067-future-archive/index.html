The lifecycle

From upload to a thousand years

Every artifact moves through the same disciplined pipeline, designed so preservation never depends on one company, one format, or one lucky backup.

Ingest

Files are fixity-checked, described with open metadata, and normalized into durable, well-documented formats.

Replicate

Independent copies fan out to vaults on separate grids and continents, verified against their checksums.

Migrate

Formats are watched for obsolescence and carried onto fresh media before anything becomes unreadable.

Access

Emulation and open readers keep works usable, with rights honored and provenance fully traceable.
