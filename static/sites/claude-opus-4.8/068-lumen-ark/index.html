    How we work

    Light first, then form.

    We begin every project by tracing the sun across the site through a full year. The plan follows the light — rooms find their orientation, apertures find their scale, and material finds its warmth.

    • 01

      Daylight modelling

      Physical and analytic studies map every hour of usable light before a single wall is drawn.

    • 02

      Quiet materials

      Lime plaster, oak and brushed concrete hold the light softly and age with grace.

    • 03

      Low-energy fabric

      Thermal mass and generous glazing balance comfort with a modest carbon footprint.
