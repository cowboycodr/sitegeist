Our materials

Nothing new is mined for this

We work only with metal that has already lived a life and stones we can trace to a single origin. Provenance travels with every piece.

Our gold, silver, and platinum are refined entirely from post-consumer and industrial scrap. Reclaimed metal is chemically identical to freshly mined — without the extraction. Every casting is poured in our own studio crucible.

Each stone arrives with documented origin — the mine, the cutter, the hands it passed through. We favour sapphires, emeralds, and diamonds with visible inclusions, because a flawless story is usually a fiction.

We leave the marks of making. A slightly uneven bezel, a stone set at its natural tilt, the tooth of a hammered surface. These are not defects to be polished away; they are the record of a human deciding when to stop.

Permanence means service, not perfection. Every Rare Earth piece can be resized, re-set, and refinished by us for life. When a piece is truly finished, we melt it back into the next one.

The pledge

Four promises stamped, in effect, on the inside of every band.

  • ReclaimedNo newly mined metal enters the studio.
  • DocumentedOrigin papers ship with every stone.
  • SingularEach design is made once, then retired.
  • ReturnableBring any piece back to be remade.

From scrap to keepsake

How pressure becomes form

Gather

Reclaimed metal and traceable rough are sorted, weighed, and matched to a sketch.

Cast

Molten metal is poured by hand into carved cuttlebone and lost-wax moulds.

Set

Stones are seated at their natural angle, bezels closed with a burnisher.

Finish

Surfaces are hammered, patinated, and hallmarked with the pledge.
