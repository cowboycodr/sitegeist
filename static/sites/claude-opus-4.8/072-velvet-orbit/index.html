Textiles with mass

Every surface is chosen for how it moves when the wearer stops.

Liquid metallic jersey
A poured, mirror-finish knit that reads as molten under raking light and settles cool against the skin.
Sculptural virgin wool
Double-faced and heat-set so tailoring holds an architectural line without any internal cage.
Weighted orbit velvet
A dense, low-nap velvet with hidden hem ballast, engineered to swing back into place.
Brushed titanium mesh
Featherlight chain-linked panels that catch and scatter light like a slow rotation.

The campaign

Shot at the farthest point of the orbit, where light arrives late and every garment becomes its own small planet. The Aphelion campaign follows six figures drifting through a room with no fixed floor.

“We wanted clothes that keep their shape when the ground gives way.”

Read the atelier notes

From the atelier

Notes on making garments that behave like bodies in motion.

Seam as gravity

We route every seam along the line a fabric would fall, then reinforce against it — the tension is what holds the silhouette.

Reflection over print

No surface print appears in Collection 04. Colour is authored entirely through how each textile reflects the room around it.

Ballast, not boning

Hidden hem weights replace traditional structure, letting garments settle rather than clamp.
