Introducing

The M1

A commuter bike distilled to its calmest form. The M1 disappears beneath you — a clean aluminium line, a whisper-quiet belt drive, and a battery folded invisibly into the frame.

◐

Silent belt drive

A carbon belt replaces the chain — no grease, no rattle. At speed the M1 reads a soft 42 dB, quieter than a passing conversation.

◇

Featherweight frame

Hydroformed aluminium keeps the whole bike at 14 kg. Lift it up a stair, hang it on a wall, carry it onto the train without a second thought.

◔

Hidden battery

72 km of assisted range tucked inside the down tube. Pop the cell out in seconds to charge it beside your desk.

◑

Adaptive assist

A torque sensor answers your legs, not a throttle. The push arrives exactly when you lean in and fades the moment you ease off.

Why quiet

Noise is a tax on the city

Engines demand attention. They fill the street with sound and the rider with tension. We designed the M1 to take nothing from the block it moves through.

  • Softer streets. No exhaust, no engine note — only the faint hum of tyres on stone.
  • A calmer ride. Less vibration and noise means you arrive composed, not rattled.
  • Room to think. Quiet is space. The M1 gives some back to every mile.

“I stopped noticing the commute and started noticing the city again.”

— Ilse M., rides the M1 across Amsterdam
