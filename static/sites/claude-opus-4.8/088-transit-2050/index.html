By 2050

Four commitments, measured in public

  • 01

    Faster than driving

    Dedicated corridors and signal priority make transit the quickest way across town at rush hour — no exceptions, no asterisks.

  • 02

    Zero tailpipe emissions

    A fully electrified fleet powered by renewable grids, cutting transport carbon to net zero and clearing the air block by block.

  • 03

    Fare that fits any budget

    Income-based fares and a free tier for essential trips, so cost never decides whether someone can reach work, care, or school.

  • 04

    Reachable by everyone

    Step-free stations, tactile wayfinding, and real-time audio arrivals — an accessible ride designed with disabled riders, not for them.

The equity case

Mobility is opportunity

Where you can go decides what you can do. Transit 2050 measures success not by the fastest trip, but by the widest reach — closing the gap between neighborhoods that have always had options and those that haven't.

Every route is scored on the jobs, clinics, and classrooms it unlocks for the people who currently wait the longest.

The roadmap

From plan to platform

  1. 2026

    Foundations

    Electrify the first corridors, launch income-based fares, and open the data so anyone can audit progress.

  2. 2032

    Backbone

    Complete the rapid rail spine and interlock every mode onto a single tap-to-ride fare.

  3. 2041

    Reach

    Extend on-demand shuttles into every underserved neighborhood, hitting a 5-minute walk citywide.

  4. 2050

    Everyone moves

    A zero-emission, always-on network where a fair ride is simply how the city works.
