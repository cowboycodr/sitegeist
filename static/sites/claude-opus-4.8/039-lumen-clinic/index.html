Questions

Good to know before you join.

Is Lumen a replacement for my doctor?

No — Lumen is preventive, not urgent care. We surface early signals and clear next steps, and we'll always tell you when something belongs with your physician. Members keep their existing providers.

Where does my blood draw happen?

At any partner lab near you, or a home visit on the Complete and Household plans. Results route back to your Lumen dashboard automatically within about 48 hours.

Who reviews my results?

Every panel is checked by a licensed clinician before guidance reaches you. The dashboard translates the data; a human confirms the interpretation.

How is my data protected?

Your records are encrypted, never sold, and yours to export or delete at any time. Sharing with a provider only happens when you choose it.
