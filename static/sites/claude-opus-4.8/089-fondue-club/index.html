House rules

Lose the bread, pay the forfeit

Drop your bread in the pot and you owe the table a story, a song, or the next bottle.

No double dipping

We are unserious, not uncivilised. One dip, one bite, no exceptions.

Everyone stirs

The pot belongs to the table. Keep it moving or it grabs the bottom.
