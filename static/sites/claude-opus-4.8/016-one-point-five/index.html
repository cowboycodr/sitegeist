    The line still matters

    Every tenth of a degree is a measurable change in heatwaves, harvests, and coastlines. 1.5 °C is not a cliff edge — it is a threshold past which the costs of adaptation climb steeply and unevenly.

    The trend is legible

    The signals move together and they move in one direction. Reading them as a group removes the noise that lets any single year be dismissed.

    The response is collective

    The trajectory chart has no single lever because the future has no single author. The steep pathways are only reachable together — and they remain reachable.
