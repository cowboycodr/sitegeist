Why a museum of small things?

Because history keeps the crowns and forgets the buttons — and it is the buttons that were sewn back on with love.

Grand museums preserve the objects of kings. This one preserves the objects of the rest of us: the drawer, the pocket, the windowsill. We believe that an ordinary thing, kept long enough and loved hard enough, becomes a kind of instrument — and that if you hold it to your ear, you can still hear the life that passed through it.
