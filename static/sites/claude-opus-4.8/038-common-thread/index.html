Traceability

Follow the thread, field to wardrobe

Every Common Thread garment carries a QR that opens this journey. Select a stage to see who made it, where, and its measured footprint.

Stage 01

Farm & fiber

Ferme du Lin · Normandy, France

Our flax is rainfed and rotated on named plots. No irrigation, no defoliants — the crop is pulled, not cut, so the whole fiber length stays intact for a stronger yarn.

Built to be mended

Designed to be repaired, not replaced

A garment's biggest footprint is the one that replaces it too soon. We build every piece to be opened up, patched, and kept in use for decades.

01

Open seams

Flat-felled and generously allowed, so a tailor can let out, take in, or fully rebuild a panel without unpicking the whole garment.

02

Matched spares

Each order ships with a thread card and fabric offcut from your exact dye batch, ready for the mend you'll need in year three.

03

Free lifetime mending

Send any Common Thread piece back and our menders return it repaired. When it's truly done, we take the fiber back for re-spinning.

The longer-story pledge

Buy less, keep it longer. If a seam fails within ten years we mend it for free — and we'll always tell you exactly what your clothes are made of.
