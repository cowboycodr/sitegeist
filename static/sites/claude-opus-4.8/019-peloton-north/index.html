The kit

Apparel measured to the metre

Designed in Oslo, tested on the routes above. Cut for the cold, marked for the dark, built to outlast the season.

Nordlys Jersey

Merino-blend base with a signal-amber back panel visible from 300 metres of low winter sun.

Fjord Shell

Three-layer softshell that sheds coastal drizzle and packs to the size of a fist in the jersey pocket.

Polar Bib

Thermal bib tights with reflective cyan piping mapped to the exact seams a following light catches.

Contour Cap

A packable cotton cap printed with the elevation profile of the Nordmarka Long. Members only.

Come north

Your first ride is a Thursday

No membership, no fee, no pressure to prove anything. Turn up to the Fjord Recovery spin, meet the club, and see whether the longer road suits you. Bikes and lights can be arranged with a day's notice.
