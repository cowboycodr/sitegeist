The system

One panel, one connector, infinite rooms.

Three ideas do all the work. Learn them once and the whole catalog becomes something you can rearrange.

  1. 01

    The plane

    A single flat panel of 18 mm birch ply or powder-coated steel. Cut, drilled, and edge-sealed — never veneered over a secret.

  2. 02

    The node

    A turned aluminium connector that clamps planes at right angles with a quarter turn. No glue, no proprietary screws, no throwaway dowels.

  3. 03

    The grid

    Every hole sits on a 32 mm rhythm, so shelves become desks become benches. Add a bay this year, take one back next year.

Honest materials

What it is, all the way through.

Baltic birch

Thirteen even plies, no voids, oiled not lacquered. The edge is the finish.

Powder-coat steel

1.5 mm folded sheet in a matte charcoal that chips honestly and touches up clean.

Turned aluminium

The signal-orange node, machined from bar stock and left unpainted inside.

Cork & felt

Natural pads and feet, replaceable by hand, compostable at end of life.

The ethos

If we can’t show you how it’s made,
we won’t sell it.

  • Reversible. Every fastener comes apart with the tool in the box.
  • Repairable. Order a single replacement plane, not a whole new object.
  • Readable. A drawing, a cut list, and a weight ship with every kit.
  • Returnable. Send panels back and we mill them into the next batch.
