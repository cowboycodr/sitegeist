The slow part

Four moves, a lot of waiting

We don't rush the microbes. Here is roughly how a jar comes together, from market bag to labelled shelf.

  1. 01

    Gather

    Whatever's ripe at the Saturday market lands on the bench first, still cold.

  2. 02

    Salt & pack

    We weigh, salt to the gram, and press everything under its own brine.

  3. 03

    Wait & taste

    Jars breathe in the cellar for weeks. We taste, note, and taste again.

  4. 04

    Date & shelf

    When a batch turns just right, it's labelled by hand and set out to sell.
