    A day at the wheel

    Studio rituals

    Nothing here is rushed. The work is mostly waiting, done in the right order.

    1. 01

      Wedge at dawn

      Fifty turns to press the air out of the clay while the kettle warms. Cold hands, warm bench, the same rhythm every morning.

    2. 02

      Centre & open

      The wheel finds true, then a thumb opens the floor. Walls rise no faster than the clay allows — push and it tears, coax and it climbs.

    3. 03

      Trim leather-hard

      A day later the foot is carved and the maker's mark is set. Curls of clay fall away until the weight feels right in the palm.

    4. 04

      Glaze & fire

      Dipped, wiped, stacked in the kiln for two long nights. The fire decides the last ten percent, and we let it.

    Colour from the ground

    The landscapes held inside every glaze

    Each glaze is mixed from ash, iron, and stone gathered on walks near the studio. A hillside after rain, a dry riverbed, the grey-green of lichen on a north wall — they all end up in a bucket, then on a pot.

    Tap a swatch to read where its colour comes from.

    Riverbed Ash — pale oatmeal broken by iron speckle, mixed from wood ash and creek silt.
