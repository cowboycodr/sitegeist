02 — The practice

Studio

We are a small studio of architects who believe reduction is generous. Removing the excess is how we leave room for people, weather, and time.

Founded in 2009 on a canal in Christianshavn, Form Haus works across the scales of a doorway and a district. Our drawings are patient; our details are honest about material and gravity.

Every project is led by a partner from first sketch to final joint. We build slowly, in close collaboration with craftspeople, and we measure success in decades rather than seasons.

03 — How we work

Approach

  1. 01

    One decisive move

    We search for the single idea a building cannot exist without, then protect it through every decision.

  2. 02

    Material honesty

    Concrete, oak, brick, and glass are left to be themselves — aged, jointed, and true.

  3. 03

    Light as structure

    Daylight is treated as a load-bearing part of the plan, drawn and measured like any wall.

  4. 04

    Made for people

    Monumental in scale, human at the hand — thresholds, seats, and shade sized to the body.
