Seasonal field notes

What the forest is doing now

Kept by our caretaker, updated with the turning of each season.

Early Summer

The chanterelles are late

A dry May pushed the flush back three weeks. We're seeing the first golden buttons under the cedars near Cabin Four.

Midsummer

Fireflies over the wet meadow

Rare this high, but the boggy south corner lit up for four nights running. Bring no lamp; let your eyes adjust.

Late Summer

Huckleberries on the lookout trail

Heavy fruit above 3,400 feet. The bears know it too — we've moved the morning walk to the lower loop.
