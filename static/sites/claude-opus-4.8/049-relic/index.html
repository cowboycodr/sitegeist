    How Relic works

    Every object arrives with a paper trail.

    We reject far more than we list. What survives is dated, examined under good light, and described with its scars intact. No object is "like new" here — it is still here, which is the point.

    1. 01

      Sourced

      Estates, closing workshops, and quiet attics — found by people who know what to look for.

    2. 02

      Vetted

      Age, maker, and materials confirmed. Repairs disclosed. Fakes turned away at the door.

    3. 03

      Kept

      Cleaned, never over-restored. It reaches you carrying the honest weather of its years.
