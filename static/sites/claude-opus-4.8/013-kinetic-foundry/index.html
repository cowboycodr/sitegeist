Capabilities

Motion intelligence, from the joint up

Dexterity is a full-stack problem. We tune the mechanics, the sensing, and the software together so movement stays fluid under real-world load.

01 / SENSE

Force & touch feedback

Torque sensing in every joint lets the machine feel contact and back off before it pushes too hard.

02 / PLAN

Whole-body trajectories

Real-time planning blends reach, balance, and clearance into a single smooth path around people and clutter.

03 / ADAPT

Swappable end-effectors

Grippers, suction, and drivers change in seconds, and the controller recalibrates payload and grip automatically.

04 / TEACH

Learn by demonstration

Guide the arm by hand once and it captures the motion — no code, no teach pendant marathons.

Built to share space

Safer work isn't a mode you switch on

Compliance is engineered into the hardware. Our machines are designed to work beside people at full speed, then soften the instant they sense you.
