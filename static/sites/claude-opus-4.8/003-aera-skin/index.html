Barrier science

Clarity begins at the surface

When the lipid barrier is intact, skin holds water, calms itself, and reflects light evenly. We formulate for that first — no stripping, no shock.

  • Barrier-identical lipids. Ceramides and cholesterol in a skin-mimicking ratio to reinforce, not just coat.
  • Tested, not overloaded. Every formula is patch- and dermatologist-reviewed at effective, gentle concentrations.
  • Fragrance-free by default. Fewer variables, fewer flare-ups — the quiet luxury of skin left undisturbed.
