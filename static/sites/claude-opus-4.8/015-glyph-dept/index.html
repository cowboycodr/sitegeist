The Studio

We are a two-desk foundry drawing type in the open. No committees, no house style forced on your words — just letters with something to say.

Every face ships with a spec sheet, hinting notes, and a perpetual desktop and web license. Trials are unlimited for real work in progress; we only ask that you license before you publish.

  • Custom axes. Commission a bespoke weight, width, or optical size range.
  • Local delivery. Fonts arrive as self-hosted files — no third-party callbacks.
  • Fair licensing. One purchase covers your whole team and updates for the life of the face.
