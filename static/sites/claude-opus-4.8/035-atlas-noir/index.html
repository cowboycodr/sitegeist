Ethos

Restraint is the luxury.

We do not sell mileage or brochures of superlatives. We remove friction until only the place remains — the long approach, the unhurried hour, the guide who has walked the valley for thirty winters.

  • One party per route. No shared vehicles, no borrowed itineraries.
  • People before permits. Access earned through relationships, not brokers.
  • Traceless passage. Every camp leaves the ground as it was found.
