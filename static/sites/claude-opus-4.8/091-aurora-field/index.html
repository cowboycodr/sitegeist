Research residencies

Two- to eight-week stays with instrument time and quiet.

Optical

The 1.0 m Cassegrain

Queue-scheduled or classical nights on our flagship reflector, with a 4k CCD and a modest filter wheel. Priority for time-domain and faint-object programs.

Wide field

All-sky & survey array

A cluster of fast astrographs for transient hunting, meteor work, and long mosaics of the galactic plane. Data reduced on site.

Open

Artist & writer nights

Non-instrument residencies for people whose work is fed by darkness. A heated dome, a cot, and nothing on the horizon.

Public nights

Small groups, warm shelter, and a guided tour of the season's sky.

  1. 21:00

    Gather & warm up

    Meet at the field house. Hot drinks, a look at the night's targets, and time for eyes to adapt.

  2. 21:40

    Naked-eye tour

    Constellations, the Milky Way's structure, and — conditions willing — the aurora overhead.

  3. 22:30

    At the eyepiece

    Planets, double stars, nebulae, and distant galaxies through the visitor telescope.

  4. 23:30

    Long exposure

    Watch a deep image build on screen as the mount tracks the turning sky.

Plan a visit

The field lies three hours north of the nearest town. Come prepared for the cold and the quiet.

  • Dress for −20 °C, even in shoulder seasons.
  • Red-light head-torch only past the gate.
  • Nights run when skies are clear; we confirm 24 hours ahead.
  • No cell coverage on site — download directions before you leave.
