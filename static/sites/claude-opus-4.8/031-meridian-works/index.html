The bench

Patient assembly, start to finish

  1. 01

    Design

    Every reference begins as a full-scale drawing, then a machined prototype we live with for months before it earns a name.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Bridges are chamfered by hand, screw heads polished, and steel heat-blued over an open flame until the color is exactly right.

  3. 03

    Assemble

    One watchmaker builds each watch from movement to bracelet, then regulates it in six positions over two weeks.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    It ships with its own timing certificate, a service pledge, and a note from the person who assembled it.
