01 — Living work

Experiments in bloom

Each project is a small ecosystem: a model, a substrate, and a question about how creativity organizes itself.

  • E-01

    Mycelia

    A diffusion model that composes by branching — proposing motifs the way roots search soil, keeping only the paths that carry nutrients.

    generative · active
  • E-02

    Pollination

    Two agents trade half-finished ideas across a shared latent field, cross-breeding sketches until a surprising hybrid takes hold.

    multi-agent · active
  • E-03

    Phototropism

    A reward-free system that simply grows toward the most novel signal, mapping the shape of a model's own curiosity.

    interpretability · seeding
  • E-04

    Compost

    Failed generations are never discarded — they decay into priors that fertilize the next season of training runs.

    data · ongoing
