How it's built

Fewer pieces, made to last longer

We'd rather sell you one jacket for a decade than ten in the same span. Here's what that looks like.

  1. 01

    Salt-cycle tested

    Every fabric runs 200+ soak-and-dry cycles in real seawater before it's approved for the line.

  2. 02

    Repairable by design

    Modular panels and standard hardware mean a snapped zip or torn cuff is a fix, not a landfill.

  3. 03

    Traceable materials

    Recycled and bluesign-approved textiles, sourced from mills we can name and visit.

  4. 04

    Free repairs, for life

    Send it back salty and worn. We mend it, log it, and return it ready for the next tide.
