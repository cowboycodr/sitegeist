How it works

From request to doorway in four steps

  1. 1

    Request

    Staff tap a destination on the robot's screen, the wall panel, or your existing dispatch system.

  2. 2

    Plan

    Relay picks the fastest safe route, reserves the elevator, and steers gently around people.

  3. 3

    Deliver

    It arrives, announces itself by name, and unlocks the bay to a PIN or badge tap.

  4. 4

    Return

    The trip is logged, and Relay heads to its next job or docks to top up its charge.
